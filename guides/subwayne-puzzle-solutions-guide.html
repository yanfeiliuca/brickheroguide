SubWayne stations are Gotham's underground fast-travel network in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight. There are 9 stations in total — Tricorner is a story-unlocked tutorial stop, while the remaining 8 are environmental lock puzzles scattered across Gotham's open world. Each one blends into its surroundings and requires a specific playable character's gadget to crack open, so the fastest way to clear all 8 is a Free Play sweep once you have unlocked Batman, Catwoman, Jim Gordon, and Robin. Solving every SubWayne puzzle unlocks its fast-travel point and counts toward 100% completion.

What Is SubWayne?

The 8 open-world SubWayne puzzles are located at Old Gotham South, Old Gotham North, Old Gotham West, The Cauldron South, The Cauldron North (ACE Chemicals), Newtown, Gotham Village (Robinson Park), and East End (Amusement Mile). Each station's puzzle is unique — battery machines, hidden levers, water pipes, and vehicle-pull mechanics all make an appearance.

Before you start: Several stations flat-out won't interact until you have the right character unlocked. If a switch or gate isn't responding, that's usually the reason — nothing here is missable, since Gotham stays fully explorable in Free Play after the story ends.

East End

No specific character required.

  1. Go to the east side of the building and smash the pile of objects to reveal a vent.
  2. Go through the vent to reach a locked room and grab the battery inside.
  3. Swing up to the rooftop of the building to find a machine, and insert the battery into it.
  4. Return to the same room from earlier and smash the box in the corner to get a handle.
  5. Take the handle to the lever near the entrance and activate it to complete the puzzle.

Old Gotham South

Requires Catwoman.

  1. Head to the east side of the building to find a row of locked battery machines behind a gate.
  2. Switch to Catwoman and use her Whip on the peg above the pillar marked with a red up arrow.
  3. Place batteries on the second and fourth machines in the row.
  4. Push the red button next to the SubWayne entrance to open it.

Old Gotham North

Requires Jim Gordon and Batman.

  1. Switch to Batman and use his Batclaw on the plug above the dancers and the radio in front of the SubWayne entrance, then pull it to open the water pipe.
  2. Scan the area with Detective Mode to locate a water tank on the rooftop, and activate it by turning the wheel.
  3. The gush of water breaks the radio and clears the dancers from the entrance.
  4. Return to the SubWayne entrance, switch to Jim Gordon, and block the now-open pipe with his Foam Sprayer.
  5. Push the button underneath the pipe to complete the puzzle.

Old Gotham West

Requires Catwoman and Batman.

  1. Aim Catwoman's Whip at the peg next to the entrance above to unlock two targets sitting on top of the crowd.
  2. Switch to Batman and hit both targets with his Batarang to disperse the crowd.
  3. Activate the now-accessible button to unlock the SubWayne station.

The Cauldron South

Requires a vehicle.

  1. While riding a vehicle, aim at the plug on the blue shipping container and pull it until the container topples over, revealing a hidden area behind it.
  2. Walk around the fallen container and take the lever handle from the nearby shed.
  3. Insert the handle into the lever to unlock a pressure plate.
  4. Drive your vehicle onto the pressure plate — this lifts the shipping container blocking the entrance.
  5. Push the button beside the entrance to finish the puzzle.

The Cauldron North / ACE Chemicals

Requires Jim Gordon and Batman. This station is tied to the "Restore Power to the Billboard" objective — you need to activate four separate switches before the SubWayne entrance opens.

  1. Go to the back of the building, where a pipe is spraying fire. Switch to Jim Gordon and use his Foam Sprayer to put it out, then press the button near the pipe to send power through the wires.
  2. Follow the now-powered wires up the side of the building to a locked case. Punch it open and press the button inside.
  3. Go to the front of the SubWayne station to find the next switch. Pull it open with Batman's Batclaw, then hit it with a Batarang.
  4. Climb onto the roof behind the billboard to find a locked container. Punch it open and press the hidden button inside.
  5. Once all four switches are active, the ACE Chemicals billboard lights up. Interact with the nearby construction worker again to complete the SubWayne unlock.
Getting stuck here? If the billboard hasn't lit up, you're missing one of the four switches. Retrace the four steps above — the most commonly missed one is the rooftop container behind the billboard itself.

Newtown

Requires Robin and Batman.

  1. Swing up to the building on top of the SubWayne entrance and switch to Robin.
  2. Interact with the contraption next to the gated battery machines to unlock the gate.
  3. Switch to Batman and take the first battery located next to the battery machines.
  4. Use X-Ray Vision in front of the machines to see which one is connected to the electrical supply, then insert the battery into the second machine from the rightmost end.
  5. Return to the corner where the first battery was found and run to the far end of the path to find a second battery.
  6. Insert the second battery on the left side of the first battery machine to complete the puzzle.

Gotham Village / Robinson Park

Requires Jim Gordon and Batman.

  1. Scan the area to find two destructible boxes and break them for buildable parts.
  2. Use the Build prompt to construct a generator locked with a plug.
  3. Hop into a vehicle and pull the plug open to reveal a red button inside — press it.
  4. Run to the east side of the building, where a fire is blocking a second button.
  5. Switch to Jim Gordon and extinguish the fire with his Foam Sprayer, then press the newly accessible button on the wall.
  6. Block the pipe with Jim Gordon's Foam Sprayer to stop the fire from returning.
  7. Switch to Batman and hit the target above with his Batarang to complete the puzzle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to solve all 8 SubWayne puzzles for 100% completion?

Yes — SubWayne station activation is tracked as part of Gotham's open-world completion, alongside district tower activation and collectible hunting. See our 100% Completion Guide for the full checklist.

What do SubWayne stations actually do?

Each solved SubWayne puzzle unlocks a new fast-travel point, letting you jump directly to that part of Gotham instead of driving or gliding across the map.

Can I solve these before unlocking all characters?

No — several stations require Catwoman, Jim Gordon, or Robin specifically. If a station's gate or switch won't interact, you likely need a character you haven't unlocked yet. Return once your full roster is available.

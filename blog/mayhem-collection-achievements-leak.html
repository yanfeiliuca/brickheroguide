Mayhem Collection Steam Achievements Leak: 12 Trophies Reveal a Joker-Mite Shop and Mysterious "Mayhem Runs"

Published: August 3, 2026 · 8 min read

With the Mayhem Collection DLC now roughly six and a half weeks out from its September 18 release, the first genuinely new details since June have arrived — not from a trailer, but from Steam's own achievement list. According to Brick Fanatics, the platform's achievement data for the expansion went live early, and it lines up twelve new trophies that confirm several features nobody had officially detailed before: a shop run by a villainous take on Bat-Mite, a hideout the player can expand, and a mode called "Mayhem Runs" that nobody outside TT Games can fully explain yet.

Where This Leak Actually Comes From

This isn't a datamine of hidden game files — it's Steam's public achievement list for the DLC, which per Brick Fanatics' July 30 report went live ahead of the DLC's own announcement trailer. That distinction matters: achievement names and descriptions are written by the developer and shipped as structured data, so unlike the costume list a separate dataminer leaked back in May (more on that below), these twelve entries carry a higher degree of confidence even though TT Games hasn't formally detailed all of them yet.

For scale, that's about a quarter of the base game's achievement count — the main game ships with 52 PS5 trophies and 51 Xbox achievements, so twelve new ones for a single DLC pack signals a meaningfully sized expansion rather than a light cosmetic add-on.

What "Mayhem Runs" Might Actually Be

The most puzzling entry is "Absolute Mayhem," which asks players to complete five "Mayhem Runs" — a term that appears nowhere else in official Mayhem Collection materials. Brick Fanatics floated a theory from Reddit user VengeanceKnight suggesting it could be a roguelite-style mode where Joker and Harley cause escalating chaos around Gotham until Batman eventually shuts them down. That reading fits neatly with several other achievements on the list: "Planning Table" objective rerolls, a "pop-up shop" for power-ups, and crew members you can "summon" in groups of up to ten all sound like systems built for repeatable, run-based play rather than a traditional linear story mission. Worth stressing: this is a theory extrapolated from achievement names, not a confirmed mode description from TT Games or WB Games.

New Features the Achievement List Confirms

Setting the "Mayhem Run" speculation aside, several achievements point to concrete additions that weren't previously detailed:

  • A Joker-Mite shop. "Well Equipped" requires buying every item from what's presumably a villainous parody of the game's existing Bat-Mite shop, staffed by a Joker-ified version of the character from Batman: The Brave and the Bold.
  • A hideout you can expand. "Base of Operations" confirms Joker and Harley get their own home base with upgrade tiers, separate from the Batcave players use in the base game.
  • The Arkham Asylum breakout is a discrete milestone. "Maximum Security" lines up directly with the Arkham Asylum Jailbreak mission already confirmed for the DLC back in June, confirming it has its own achievement rather than being folded into a broader story trophy.
  • A.R.G.U.S. agents are the DLC's enemy force. "Lacking Agency" requires defeating 300 A.R.G.U.S. Agents — the first official confirmation that ARGUS, the government agency behind the Suicide Squad in DC lore, is involved in the Mayhem Collection's conflict.
Why ARGUS matters: Back in May, a dataminer going by X0X_LEAK posted an unverified leak claiming a roster of "Task Force X goons" — named Suicide Squad members like Deadshot, Deathstroke, and King Shark — would appear as enemies in the DLC. The achievement list's ARGUS reference is the first officially-sourced detail that lines up with that rumor, though it confirms only the ARGUS faction generally, not any specific named villain.

The Villy Awards: A Deep-Cut Nod to HBO's Harley Quinn

The "Villified" achievement — attend the Villy Awards — is a direct reference to the award ceremony Harley Quinn and Poison Ivy attend after being nominated for "Best Couple" in Season 3 of HBO's animated Harley Quinn series, per Brick Fanatics. It's a small detail, but it confirms the Mayhem Collection is drawing character material from beyond the films and comics — consistent with the May costume leak, which listed a "Villy (Villy Awards)" outfit among Joker's rumored skins for the DLC. Seeing the same specific reference surface independently in both an unverified May datamine and a July achievement list is a reasonably strong signal that the costume leak's broader strokes are accurate, even if individual costume names on that list remain unconfirmed by TT Games.

Six and a Half Weeks to Go

As of this article's publish date, the Mayhem Collection is 46 days away from its September 18 launch, which also doubles as the Switch 2 version's release date. With achievement data already live, expect TT Games or WB Games to start confirming some of these leaked details officially in the coming weeks — the Villy Awards reference alone suggests there's more character-specific content still to reveal.

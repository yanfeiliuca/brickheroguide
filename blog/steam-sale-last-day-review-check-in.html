Last Call: LEGO Batman Legacy's 20% Steam Sale Ends Today

Published: August 10, 2026 · 6 min read · Deals & Data

We've been tracking this sale for eleven days now. It first went live on July 30, briefly looked stuck at full price on Steam's own page on July 31, and was confirmed fixed by August 1. Today, August 10, is the day it closes on Steam — three days ahead of the identical discount on PlayStation Store and Xbox Store, which ComicBook.com reported runs through August 13. Checking the storefront directly this morning, the promotion is still live, so this is genuinely the last day to catch it on PC.

What's Still Discounted, As of This Morning

Pulling Steam's product page directly today, the banner still reads "SPECIAL PROMOTION! Offer ends August 10" above the buy box, with the same -20% cut that's been running since July 30. Nothing has changed on the numbers side since our last check — the discount is holding steady across all three purchasable SKUs right up to the deadline.

PS5 and Xbox buyers get more time: ComicBook.com's original coverage of this sale noted the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store versions of the same 20% discount run through August 13 — three days longer than the Steam window closing tonight. If you're on console, there's no need to rush a purchase today specifically.

The Deluxe Edition is the one worth thinking through before the discount disappears: at $71.99 today versus $89.99 at full price, it bundles the base game with the Legacy Collection's three launch-day cosmetic packs, and it's also the version that will receive the Mayhem Collection DLC (Joker and Harley Quinn playable, a new Arkham Asylum story mission, and Mayhem Mode) when that content lands September 18 at no extra cost to Deluxe owners.

Review Numbers, Nine Days Since Our Last Check

We also refreshed the review totals while the store page was open. As of today, Steam lists 13,072 total reviews across all languages (12,510 positive, 562 negative), rated Overwhelmingly Positive overall. That's up from the 12,903 total reviews we recorded on August 1, a gain of about 169 reviews over nine days. The English-language subset now sits at 6,293 reviews, also Overwhelmingly Positive, up slightly from 6,218 on August 1.

The recent-reviews window, which covers roughly the last 30 days, now shows Very Positive at 90% of 459 reviews. That's a touch below the 94% of 688 we saw back on July 26, and also below the 92% of 489 recorded on August 1 — a gradual, mild softening in the most recent sample rather than any sharp drop. For a game that's nearly three months past launch, settling into the high-80s/low-90s range on recent reviews while the lifetime score holds at Overwhelmingly Positive is a normal pattern, not a red flag.

Should You Buy Before Tonight?

If you've been sitting on the fence specifically because of the Steam window, today is the deadline: the -20% pricing on Standard, Deluxe, and the Deluxe Upgrade DLC all revert to full price once the promotion ends. Console buyers have until August 13 on PlayStation Store and Xbox Store per ComicBook.com's reporting, so there's no equivalent urgency there today. Either way, the review math backs up the purchase — an 84 Metacritic score, a 95%-plus lifetime positive rate on Steam, and an OpenCritic "Mighty" tier rating are hard numbers to argue with at $55.99, let alone $69.99.

For anyone still deciding between Standard and Deluxe, our Deluxe Edition breakdown lays out exactly what's included today versus what arrives free with the September 18 Mayhem Collection.

Joker's Parade: Every Cake, WayneTech Cache & the Showbiz Red Brick

Published: August 17, 2026 · 6 min read

Chapter 2's climactic mission drops Batman and Catwoman into Joker's street parade, and unlike some of the game's sprawling open districts, this one is deceptively compact — a single street lined with three parade floats (a rocket, a volcano, and a dragon), a handful of alleyways, and the apartment buildings overlooking them. That tight footprint is exactly why the collectibles here are so easy to miss on a first pass: five cakes, five WayneTech Caches, and one Red Brick are all packed into a level most players clear in under fifteen minutes. This site's boss fights guide already covers the parade's floats and balloon-release sequence at a high level; this piece is the dedicated collectible-by-collectible map that's been missing.

Every Cake Location

The Batcave trophy for this mission tracks five cakes scattered around food stalls, floats, and back alleys.

  1. Cake 1: Right at the start, hop along the four jump points, turn right at the bottom, and the first cake is sitting behind a van.
  2. Cake 2: Above a groaning WayneTech employee to the left of the first float is a suspended cradle — grapple up to find the second cake.
  3. Cake 3: Sitting in plain sight on a food stall counter to the right of the second float.
  4. Cake 4: Hidden on the back of the volcano float — easy to walk past if you're only looking at the front.
  5. Cake 5: Tucked behind two dumpsters in an alleyway to the left of the second float.

Every WayneTech Cache

The five caches lean more on Catwoman's toolkit than the cakes do, with a couple of multi-step puzzles worth knowing in advance.

  1. Cache 1: Head left of the first float into the alleyway, climb the wall, and pull the lever to open the building's shutters. Inside, look right for a cat flap and send a cat through to pull a second lever, which opens an interior door. Return with Batman and Catwoman to open the cache.
  2. Cache 2: Just ahead of the first float, a WayneTech employee is grumbling about losing company tech again. Use the UV vision prompt, follow the footsteps into the alleyway, and pull back the panel to reveal the cache.
  3. Cache 3: To the left of the second float is a ledge — grapple up, then grapple again to a window. Break in with Catwoman and the cache is inside the apartment.
  4. Cache 4: Hidden inside a Gotham Globe news van to the right of the third float. Pull the grapple point with Batman to pop the back doors and the cache spills out.
  5. Cache 5: As Catwoman, cut a hole in the window left of the third float at the end of the street, jump through, and take out the security cameras before grabbing the cache — miss the cameras and the shutters at the back will temporarily seal shut.
Missed one? This is a linear story mission, so once it ends you can't wander back in to mop up a missed cake or cache — you'll need to replay it from the Batcave computer's mission-select screen. Worth clearing all ten collectibles in one pass before you move on.

The Red Brick: Showbiz Modifier

The Red Brick in this mission isn't just a trophy line item — it unlocks the "Showbiz" cosmetic modifier, which swaps in a parade-themed color palette for the Batmobile once equipped from the Batcave workbench.

Between the second and third floats, look up and to the right for a zipwire. Grapple up and jump from the zipwire onto a window box, then cut a hole in the window to get inside. Once in the apartment, pull the TV out halfway between the bench and the shelves, summon a cat, and jump from the bench to the TV to the shelves to reach a vent. Crawl through it into a small living room — complete with its own cat tree — where the Red Brick is sitting in plain view.

Wrapping Up the Mission

Once all three floats' release mechanisms are triggered — the rocket's wheel is hidden in a cat vent below its float, the volcano opens by spinning the towers in front of it, and the dragon float needs Batman's X-Ray Vision to find its gate release before Catwoman cracks the safe inside — the balloons go up, Joker's henchmen swarm in, and the mission wraps with a build sequence for your own balloon. That's the cue that Chapter 2 is over and the story moves on toward Chapter 3's Two-Face and Poison Ivy content.

If you're working through the game chapter by chapter for 100% completion, this mission is also a good checkpoint to swing by the WayneTech upgrades guide — five more caches banked here puts you that much closer to the early unlock milestones. And if cosmetic unlocks like the Showbiz Batmobile are your thing, the full Red Bricks guide rounds up every other modifier the campaign hands out.

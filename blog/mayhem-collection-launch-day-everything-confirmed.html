Mayhem Collection Is Out Now: Everything Confirmed for LEGO Batman's Double Launch Day

📅 September 18, 2026  |  ⏱ 7 min read

This site has been counting down to today since early August — the reveal, the Gamescom trailer, the designer interview. As of today, September 18, there's nothing left to confirm: the Mayhem Collection DLC is live on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, and the brand-new Nintendo Switch 2 version of the base game, and all of it launched on the exact same day. Here's a clean recap of what's actually in your hands right now, including a few final details Warner Bros. Games only locked down in the last 24–48 hours.

New since our last update (August 30): the full outfit count for Joker and Harley themselves (30 pieces, separate from the 7-suit Sinister Pack), the Joker-Mite shop concept, the Henchmaster's comic origin, and the exact Switch 2 Deluxe Edition launch bundle are all confirmed for the first time below.

How to Get It Today

If you already own the Deluxe Edition of LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on any platform, the Mayhem Collection unlocked automatically today — no extra download or purchase needed. Standard Edition owners can buy the Deluxe Edition Upgrade for $24.99 / £21.99 / €24.99, which bundles in both the Mayhem Collection and the earlier Legacy Collection DLC in one purchase.

Mayhem Mode, Explained

The headline feature is Mayhem Mode: repeatable, time-limited runs as The Joker and Harley Quinn across a locked-down version of the game's North Island. While the clock is running, you can rob shops, smash ATMs, paint graffiti, steal vehicles, and fight A.R.G.U.S. agents — then you have to make it back to the duo's customizable Funhouse hideout on Amusement Mile before Task Force X (the Suicide Squad) catches up and drags you back to Arkham Asylum. It sits alongside a separate, one-time Story Mission where Joker and Harley orchestrate their actual breakout from Arkham.

A hands-on preview from Gamescom, published before launch, described the two villains as genuinely distinct to play — Harley's hammer doubles as a tool for smashing through obstacles too tough for a standard takedown, while Joker leans on a boxing-glove gun and a paint-based gadget to weaponize the environment and stage getaways. The two also have combo moments built around exploration, such as Harley prying open something too heavy to move alone so Joker can access what's inside.

The Sinister Pack, Joker-Mite Shops & the Henchmaster

The Sinister Pack remains the core cosmetic reward: seven new Joker-themed suits (one for each of the base game's playable characters, each tied to a real DC comic issue), a Jokermobile built on the Arkham trilogy's design language, and five Batcave customization props. Beyond that pack, Warner Bros. Games confirms Joker and Harley themselves get a much larger wardrobe — 30 additional unlockable outfits, plus props and trophies purchasable with studs earned in Mayhem Mode.

Two new NPCs round out the DLC's cast. Joker-Mite is a fifth-dimensional imp who runs pop-up shops across Gotham selling weapons, gadgets, and trophies — a mirror image of Bat-Mite's own retail habit in the comics. The Henchmaster, a character first introduced in the comic Batman: The Joker War Zone #1, trains any Arkham inmates you rescue during your runs so they can be summoned to fight alongside you as part of a customizable crew. Other familiar DC faces — Amanda Waller, Mad Hatter, Poison Ivy, Bronze Tiger, and more Task Force X members — also turn up across the DLC's pre-release footage.

A Batman: The Animated Series Love Letter

Per TT Games Design Director Jimmy Sedora, the Mayhem Collection leans deliberately into Batman: The Animated Series — the 1992 show where Harley Quinn made her debut — right down to rendering the DLC's cutscenes in a 4:3 aspect ratio as a visual nod to the original broadcast. It's a detail aimed squarely at longtime Batman fans rather than anything that changes moment-to-moment gameplay, but it explains a lot of the DLC's visual tone if you've noticed it already.

The Other Half of Today: Switch 2

Running in parallel, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight itself is now available on Nintendo Switch 2 for the first time, priced at $69.99 (Standard) and $89.99 (Deluxe). Switch 2 Deluxe Edition owners get the Mayhem Collection, the earlier Legacy Collection DLC, and — for physical copies while supplies last — a free LEGO Retro Video Game Batman minifigure, all bundled in at launch. Every Switch 2 preorder, Standard or Deluxe, also unlocks The Dark Knight Returns Batsuit today — consistent with what this site has confirmed since the base game's May launch, this suit was never platform-exclusive, and Switch 2 owners are simply catching up to the same reward PS5, Xbox, and PC players claimed back in May.

As for how the Switch 2 port actually runs, that's the one thing nobody outside TT Games can confirm yet: performance breakdowns and reviews for the new platform are only starting to roll in today, now that the version is actually in players' hands.

Bonus: A Real-World Batman Day Pop-Up

If you're in New York City, Warner Bros. Games is marking Batman Day 2026 with a pop-up at the Microsoft Experience Center (667 Fifth Avenue) tomorrow, September 19, from 12–5pm EST, where visitors can try the Mayhem Collection in person and pick up free promotional items.

Bottom line: after months of leaks, trailers, and designer interviews, everything about the Mayhem Collection is now playable rather than promised.

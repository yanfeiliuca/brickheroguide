AR (Augmented Reality) Trials are Gotham's open-world skill challenges, and there are three distinct types spread across every district: Combat, Traversal, and Driving. Between them, there are 28 total AR Trials in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight. They're introduced during the main story in Chapter 3, in the Kite Man investigation's "Dynamic Duo" mission, and remain available afterward through Free Play. This guide covers what each type actually asks of you, where to find them, and how to walk away with a Gold Medal instead of a Bronze one.

What Are AR Trials?

Each of Gotham's districts hosts a mix of the three trial types. Combat Trials task you with fighting off three waves of increasingly difficult enemies within a time limit. Traversal Trials have you race through a course of glowing rings using jumping, sprinting, gliding, and grappling. Driving Trials are the vehicle equivalent — hit a series of checkpoint gates while boosting and drifting through a set route. All three reward Studs on completion, with a bigger payout the faster you clear them.

Combat Trials

There are 10 Combat Trials in total, spread roughly one per district — East End's Amusement Mile is the exception, holding two. Each trial drops you into an arena and locks the exits until you've cleared three waves of enemies, with tougher enemy types appearing as the waves progress.

Wave-clear priority order: Deal with special enemies first — their attacks are harder to counter or evade than regular ones and will disrupt your combo if ignored. Handle ranged enemies next, since their attacks can only be dodged and will interrupt a combo if they land. Standard melee enemies last; counter their attacks the instant they strike to parry, stun, and unleash a flurry of punches while building Focus faster than normal attacking does.

The Stud gap between a Bronze and a Gold clear on a Combat Trial is roughly 7,000 Studs per run, so it's worth replaying ones you rushed through early on once your combat kit is stronger.

Traversal Trials

There are 10 Traversal Trials, one per district except Tricorner, which has two (east and west). The confirmed locations are:

  • Tricorner (east)
  • Tricorner (west)
  • Old Gotham South
  • Old Gotham North
  • Old Gotham West
  • The Cauldron South
  • The Cauldron North — ACE Chemicals
  • Newtown
  • Gotham Village — Robinson Park
  • East End — Amusement Mile

Each course is a fixed racetrack of rings that you clear using whatever mix of jumping, sprinting, gliding, and grappling gets you through fastest. In the Tricorner trial, Gold requires a time of 01:10.00 for 10,000 Studs, Silver requires 02:00.00 for 5,000 Studs, and Bronze requires 05:00.00 for 3,000 Studs — the same tier structure applies across the other nine courses, though exact times vary by course length.

Getting Gold: Run the course once at a relaxed pace to memorize the ring order and any shortcuts, then attempt your timed run once you know exactly where you're going. Traversal Trials punish hesitation more than mistakes — a wrong turn costs more time than a missed ring.

Driving Trials

Driving Trials are the vehicle-based version of the same challenge format: you drive a fixed route, boosting and drifting through a series of gates within a time limit. One confirmed location is just south of the GCPD building in Tricorner. Working backward from the game's total of 28 AR Trials (10 Combat + 10 Traversal), there are 8 Driving Trials spread across the remaining districts — we haven't been able to source a complete district-by-district list for all 8 yet, so treat that count as a total rather than a confirmed location list, and check each district as you explore.

Gold Medal on a Driving Trial pays 10,000 Studs, Silver pays 5,000, and Bronze pays 3,000 — the same reward structure as Traversal Trials.

Boost technique: Vehicles have a Boost that you can hold down for a sustained speed increase. Boost continuously on straight stretches leading into the next gate rather than tapping it — holding the button is faster than pulsing it, and it's the single biggest factor in hitting Gold times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Gold on every AR Trial for 100% completion?

Completion tracking generally requires clearing each trial at least once rather than requiring Gold specifically, but Gold Medals are commonly tied to separate Stud-farming and trophy goals. Check our Trophy & Achievement Guide for the exact requirements tied to Batcave challenges.

Can I retry an AR Trial after clearing it?

Yes. All three trial types remain available after your first clear, so you can return in Free Play to upgrade a Bronze or Silver result to Gold once you have stronger traversal or combat upgrades.

Which trial type is fastest to farm Studs with?

Traversal Trials tend to have the shortest completion times once memorized, making them efficient for repeat Gold runs if your traversal upgrades (grapple range, wall-run distance) are already maxed. See our WayneTech Upgrades Guide for the priority order.

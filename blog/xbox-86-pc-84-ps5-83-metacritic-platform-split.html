Xbox 86, PC 84, PS5 83: LEGO Batman Legacy's Metacritic Scores Finally Split by Platform

📅 August 14, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

Three months after launch, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight's review picture has quietly gotten more interesting. This session checked both Metacritic and Steam directly on August 14, 2026, and found two things worth reporting: Metacritic's critic score has finally split out by platform — something the site's earlier "critic scores hold steady" coverage couldn't show because the aggregate figures hadn't diverged yet — and Steam's review count has pushed past 13,000 just as the summer's first sale wraps up everywhere.

Sale status check: As of today, the game is back to full price on Steam — $69.99 standalone, $89.99 for the Deluxe bundle, $24.99 for the Deluxe Edition Upgrade alone. That matches what we tracked in our Deluxe Edition guide: the 20% discount ran through August 10 on Steam and August 13 on PlayStation and Xbox, so today is the first day the whole game has been back at list price across every storefront.

Metacritic Splits by Platform for the First Time

Up to now, this site has generally referenced Metacritic's overall figures. Checking the game's Metacritic page directly today shows critic scores have separated out per platform, and they're not identical:

PlatformMetascoreCritic Reviews Counted
Xbox Series X8616
PC8423
PlayStation 58385

Worth reading with a little caution: PS5 has by far the largest critic sample (85 reviews) and lands at 83, "Generally Favorable" on Metacritic's scale. PC sits a couple of points higher at 84 across 23 reviews. Xbox Series X posts the highest number at 86, but that's built on only 16 critic reviews — a small enough sample that it can move with just one or two additional scores. None of the three platforms have moved into "Universal Acclaim" territory, and none have slipped out of favorable ranges either; the spread mostly reflects which outlets reviewed which version at launch rather than any meaningful difference in the game itself, since it's the same build across platforms.

Metacritic's PS5 user score sits at 8.4 out of 10 across 627 user ratings (83% positive, 10% mixed, 7% negative) — consistent with the critic consensus rather than the more polarized user-vs-critic gap you sometimes see on other LEGO or superhero titles.

Steam Crosses 13,000 Reviews

Our July 26 check-in put Steam's all-language review count at 12,665. Checking the store page again today puts that figure at 13,199 — a gain of 534 reviews in 19 days, or roughly 28 a day. The breakdown:

  • 12,629 positive / 570 negative across all languages and purchase types — a 95.7% positive rate, comfortably inside Steam's "Overwhelmingly Positive" band.
  • Steam-verified purchasers: 8,391 reviews, up from 8,072 on July 26.
  • Other purchase types (bundle keys, gifts, non-Steam activations, etc.): 4,808, up from 4,593.
  • Last 30 days only: 464 reviews at 91% positive — still "Very Positive," just a notch below the all-time average, which is normal for a game three months past its launch spike.

English-language reviews alone account for 6,339 of the total, rated "Overwhelmingly Positive" at 95%.

Who's Playing It: An International Snapshot

Steam only generates a separate review score for languages with enough volume to be statistically meaningful, and today's page shows five language groups have crossed that threshold — the first time this site has been able to report which international communities have grown large enough to register individually:

LanguageReview CountRating
English6,339Overwhelmingly Positive
Portuguese (Brazil)335Very Positive

Simplified Chinese291Very Positive

None of the four non-English groups have crossed into "Overwhelmingly Positive" territory yet — each needs a higher volume and consistency of positive reviews to clear that bar — but all four are solidly "Very Positive," and their combined total (1,159 reviews) is now large enough that Steam breaks them out individually rather than lumping them into "All Languages." It's a small but genuine signal that the game's audience outside English-speaking markets has kept growing well past launch week.

One quiet consistency check: Steam's achievement list for this game shows exactly 52 unlockable achievements — matching the PS5 trophy count in our own Trophy Guide data. Xbox's equivalent list sits at 51, one fewer than PlayStation, which lines up with what we've had documented since launch.

What's Next

The next real inflection point for these numbers is September 18, when the Mayhem Collection DLC and the Nintendo Switch 2 version both launch on the same day. A new platform entering the review pool, plus a fresh wave of players picking the game up for Joker and Harley Quinn, should move both the Metacritic and Steam figures again. We'll check back in once that lands — see our Mayhem Collection DLC guide for what's confirmed so far, and our PC Requirements guide if you're deciding which platform to grab it on.

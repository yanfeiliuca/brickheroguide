Mr. Freeze Boss Fight: The Complete Shield, Hack & Crank Strategy Guide

📅 August 13, 2026  |  ⏱ 7 min read

More players get stuck on Mr. Freeze than any other Chapter 4 encounter, and the reason is almost always the same: they treat it like a normal boss fight and try to punch him down. Mr. Freeze isn't a damage race. The entire mission is built around one objective — shutting down his giant ice machine — and the actual damage you land on him is secondary to solving that machine, phase by phase. This guide breaks down the fight structure, his attack patterns, the Dark Knight difficulty adjustments, and the Snow Globe and Red Brick locations you can only grab once he's down.

Why this fight has history: Mr. Freeze is also the boss encounter behind one of the game's most notable early bugs. Update 1.006 (June 2, 2026) fixed a crash during this exact fight that was blocking the story trophy from unlocking on PS5 and Xbox. If you're replaying this mission on console and haven't updated in a while, make sure 1.006 or later is installed — see our Trophy Guide for the full patch note.

This Is a Puzzle Fight, Not a Damage Race

The Mr. Freeze mission is the climax of Chapter 4 and the entire level is the boss encounter — there's no separate approach section beforehand. Batman starts the fight alone, but partway through he gets frozen solid, and that's the cue for Nightwing to join the fight and take over the machine-focused objectives. This is also the moment Nightwing formally joins your roster as a playable character going forward, with his Electric Cable Launcher and wall-jump abilities carried over into the rest of the game. The fight is built around three phases: an opening fight where you're mostly surviving Mr. Freeze and his goons, a shield phase where you have to physically disable the machine's energy shield, and a shutdown phase where you hack, climb, and crank the machine into failure.

Mr. Freeze's Attack Patterns

None of his attacks are especially fast, but they punish players who stand still or stay directly in front of him. Learn these four and the fight gets much calmer:

AttackHow to Avoid ItBest Counter Window
Freeze Ray sweepMove sideways — don't stand directly in front of him when he raises the weapon.After the beam ends or during the interrupt window the fight gives you.
Ice projectile bombardmentWatch the ground markers and keep moving instead of standing still to fight henchmen.The pause between volleys — use it to clear enemies or reach the next machine objective.
Ice shockwaveBack away as he charges it, then jump the expanding wave rather than trying to roll through it.Right after the wave finishes expanding.
Hover slamWatch the landing marker and dash out before he crashes down.His recovery right after landing.

The Freeze Ray is the one to respect most. Keep moving sideways rather than trying to force melee combos while he's actively aiming it.

Phase-by-Phase Walkthrough

Phase 1: Disable the Energy Shield

Once the objective shifts to the energy shield, stop chasing Mr. Freeze around the arena. Clear just enough henchmen to safely reach the build area near the shield objective, then break the nearby LEGO pieces to assemble a generator. Connect the generator to the machine system to overload the shield — the goal here is disabling the shield, not damaging the machine directly. As soon as the shield drops, move to the computer hack point before enemy pressure pulls you back into a fight you don't need to have.

Phase 2: Hack, Climb, Crank

With the shield down, use the computer to hack the machine. The hack lowers it and exposes a manual shutdown route — climb onto the newly exposed section and use the crank interaction to keep the sequence moving.

Phase 3: Icicles and the Final Crank

The last phase introduces hanging icicles as cable-launcher targets. This is where players who are still trying to just beat Mr. Freeze down tend to lose track of the objective. Use the cable launcher on the icicles when the fight points you toward them, then return to the machine for the final crank sequence, which ends the encounter. Don't get greedy chasing extra hits here — clear only what's blocking your path to the crank and finish the interaction.

Dark Knight Difficulty Adjustments

On Dark Knight difficulty, the same fight gets noticeably less forgiving: the Freeze Ray tracks your movement more aggressively, and the objective windows between attacks feel shorter.

  • Beating the Freeze Ray tracking: don't run in a straight line. Move in a small curve, then reverse direction to break the beam's tracking.
  • AoE ground attacks: gliding or an extended jump clears the shockwave more reliably than rolling on this difficulty.
  • Henchmen interrupting objectives: only clear the enemies standing between you and the generator, hack point, crank, or cable launcher — ignore the rest.
  • Losing the thread of the fight: use Mr. Freeze's recovery windows to re-check the arena. This is a puzzle fight with combat pressure layered on top, not a pure DPS check.

Snow Globes and the Red Brick (Collect These After the Fight)

All of the mission's collectibles — 5 Snow Globes and 1 Red Brick — are meant to be picked up after Mr. Freeze is defeated, once the arena is calm. There's no WayneTech Cache in this mission, so those two collectible types are the whole checklist. Per our Chapter 4 walkthrough, Snow Globes #1 and #5 sit on the left side of the machine and behind it, #2 is on the ledge behind your starting position, #3 is on the wall to your left after the fight, and #4 is on the right side of the arena — all five are grabbable with just Batman and Nightwing, no special gadget required.

The Red Brick needs both characters working together: use Batgirl's hacking ability on the wall behind your starting position to reveal three switchboard targets, then switch to Nightwing and hit each one with his birdarang. One of the three is guarded behind a pair of grates, so clear that panel with a hackarang throw first before the birdarang can reach it. Once all three are hit, the glass box holding the Red Brick pops open. It's fully obtainable on your first playthrough, since Batgirl and Nightwing are both already in your roster by this point in the story. Worth remembering: Red Bricks in this game are cosmetic colour-skin unlocks, not Stud multipliers or gameplay modifiers — see our Collectibles Guide for the complete list of what each one unlocks.

Bottom line: Treat Mr. Freeze as a machine puzzle with combat pressure attached, not a boxing match. Learn the four attack patterns, work the shield-hack-crank sequence in order, and save the Snow Globe and Red Brick sweep for after he's down.

Mystery Solved: LEGO Batman Legacy's 20% Sale Is Now Showing on Steam's Own Page

Published: August 1, 2026 · 6 min read · Deals & Data

For two days running, we reported something odd: third-party trackers said LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight was 20% off on Steam, but every time we pulled up Steam's own storefront page directly, it showed full price with no discount banner. We first flagged the sale on July 30 and documented the discrepancy again on July 31. Checking a third time today, the picture has changed: Steam's page now shows the discount clearly, unambiguously, on every SKU.

What Changed Today

Fetching the store page directly this morning, the page's own title now reads "Save 20% on LEGO® Batman™: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Steam" — a change from the plain, no-sale title we saw on both prior checks. The page itself displays a "SPECIAL PROMOTION! Offer ends August 10" banner directly above the price, with the Standard Edition struck through from $69.99 down to $55.99. There's no ambiguity this time: the -20% tag is sitting right on the buy box.

Cross-checked independently: We also re-pulled GG.deals' listing today, and it still matches exactly — "Regular price: $69.99, Discount: -20%, Current price: $55.99." For the first time since we started tracking this, our direct Steam check and the third-party tracker agree.

All Three SKUs, Confirmed at the Same 20%

The discount isn't limited to the base game. Every purchase option tied to LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Steam is showing the same -20% cut as of today's check:

Edition / ItemRegular PriceSale PriceOffer Ends

Deluxe Edition (base game + Deluxe Upgrade)$89.99$71.99August 10

That's consistent with what ComicBook.com and GG.deals originally reported on July 28–29, and it now matches what Steam's own page shows too. Our earlier reporting also noted the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store discounts were said to run through August 13 — three days longer than Steam's window — though we haven't independently re-verified those two storefronts today.

So What Was Going On for Two Days?

We still can't say for certain why our direct fetches of Steam's page showed full price on July 30 and July 31 while GG.deals' listing was already live. The honest possibilities are the same ones we named at the time: regional or account-based pricing differences, a delayed rollout of the promotional banner on Valve's end, or some kind of caching quirk in how the page was served to us on those specific checks. What we can say is that the gap has now closed — three consecutive daily checks, and today is the first time all our sources point to the exact same price.

Review Numbers, Six Days Later

While we had the store page open, we also pulled a fresh read on the review totals. Steam now shows 12,903 total reviews (12,359 positive, 544 negative), up from the 12,665 reviews recorded in our last full check on July 26. That's a gain of roughly 238 reviews in six days, and the overall positive rate held essentially steady at 95.8% — still comfortably inside the "Overwhelmingly Positive" tier. The English-language subset alone sits at 6,218 reviews, also rated Overwhelmingly Positive.

The one softer number is the "recent reviews" window: Steam currently labels the last 30 days as Very Positive at 92% of 489 reviews, down from the 94% of 688 reviews we recorded on July 26. That's a smaller and slightly less enthusiastic recent sample than a week ago, which tracks with a two-month-old game settling into a normal post-launch review cadence rather than any specific new complaint we could identify. It's still a strong score by any standard — just not quite as hot as the review pace right around the first big sale announcement.

Should You Buy Now?

If you were holding off because our checks over the past two days couldn't confirm the price, that uncertainty is gone: as of today, Steam's own page, at checkout box level, shows $55.99 for the Standard Edition and $71.99 for Deluxe, both expiring August 10. If you're deciding between editions, our Deluxe Edition breakdown covers exactly what the Legacy Collection includes now and what the September 18 Mayhem Collection will add later. As always with time-limited storefront promotions, it's still worth glancing at your own checkout screen before completing a purchase, but the multi-day discrepancy that prompted this mini-series of posts appears to be resolved.

Poison Ivy's Botanical Gardens: All 5 Venus Fly Traps, 5 WayneTech Caches & the Flower Power Red Brick

📅 August 18, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

The Botanical Gardens mission that closes out Dr. Pamela Isley's transformation into Poison Ivy is one of the more collectible-dense levels in Chapter 3, and it's easy to walk straight past most of it — the leaf-jumping puzzles and vine mazes keep your eyes on platforming, not side rooms. Between the greenhouse entrance and Isley's lab, the level hides 5 Venus Fly Traps, 5 WayneTech Caches, and 1 Red Brick, for 11 collectibles total. This guide walks through where to find all of them, in roughly the order you'll reach them during the mission.

Before you start: Everything here is missable but nothing is permanently lost — replay the mission any time from the Batcomputer in the Batcave if you want a clean collectible run. The Venus Fly Trap set doubles as a Batcave trophy once you've grabbed all five, and the WayneTech Caches feed directly into your ability-point pool, so it's worth the extra five minutes on a first playthrough.

All 5 Venus Fly Trap Locations

Venus Fly Traps are the level's Batcave trophy collectible — grab all five and they unlock as a decorative model back at the Batcave.

  1. Trap 1: Right at the start of the level. Climb through the window above the door and turn right into the greenhouse to spot it immediately.
  2. Trap 2: After you drop down from the ledge to fight the Terra-Troopers, it's directly behind your landing spot.
  3. Trap 3: Past the scientist conversation, use the cable launcher to cross the giant leaves over the gas pit. The trap sits to the right of the latch point at the end of that hallway.
  4. Trap 4: Near the double doors Batman has to pull open with his grapple hook (shortly before the Red Brick room) — it's just to the right of that door.
  5. Trap 5: Saved for the very end: build the plow machine near Isley's lab and drive it straight through the wall of plants to reveal the last trap underneath.

All 5 WayneTech Cache Locations

The caches are spread out more than the fly traps and a couple require a specific gadget interaction, so keep this order in mind as you play:

  1. Cache 1: At the mission's opening waterfall, grapple the rock sitting on top of it to dislodge it and dam the flow. With the water stopped, head behind the waterfall and cross the bridge to reach the cache.
  2. Cache 2: Before talking to the scared scientist, throw a birdarang at the three plant targets nearby to raise three platforms. Climb up to find the cache waiting on the ledge above.
  3. Cache 3: After the scientist conversation, cross the gas hallway and survive the Terra-Trooper ambush. Instead of continuing toward the lab, go left around the corner (watch for an explosive plant) to find the cache.
  4. Cache 4: In the platforming section just past the double doors Batman grapples open, the cache sits underneath the first slamming vine trap.
  5. Cache 5: Continue past the two vines blocking the doorway and hop along the venus fly trap platforms (don't get chomped). At the halfway point, turn around and use Robin's cable launcher on the window behind you, then grapple through to the final cache.

The Red Brick — Flower Power

Partway through the level, at the same double doors where you find the fourth Venus Fly Trap, look left instead of right. The Red Brick sits in a locked case flanked by two pressure plates, with three plant pots nearby. Place the green plant on the left plate and the red plant on the right plate to pop the case open. Like the rest of this game's Red Bricks, Flower Power is a cosmetic modifier rather than a gameplay changer — toggling it on from the Batcave applies a pastel, floral colour palette to the Batmobile, in keeping with the greenhouse theme of the level it's found in.

Order can vary slightly: A couple of independent playthrough guides describe interacting with the pressure-plate puzzle in a different plant order before it accepted the solution, so if your first placement doesn't pop the case, try swapping which plant goes on which plate — the puzzle appears to accept more than one valid combination.

Why Bother Collecting All 11

None of these are required to finish the mission, but they compound with the rest of the game's collection systems. WayneTech Caches count toward the milestone rewards tracked across all 200 caches in the game (unlocks trigger at 10, 30, 50, 70, 80, 90, 110, 140, 150, 160, and 180 caches collected) — see our WayneTech Upgrades Guide for the full milestone list. The Venus Fly Trap set is one of several themed Batcave trophy collections spread across the game's 247+ total collectibles, covered in full in our Collectibles Guide. And if you're chasing platinum or 100% completion, missable Red Bricks like Flower Power are exactly the kind of thing that's easy to forget until a completion checklist flags it — our Trophy Guide covers which collectible categories actually gate trophies and which are purely cosmetic.

Bottom line: Botanical Gardens rewards a slow first pass more than most Chapter 3 missions. Grab the fly traps as you naturally pass them, detour left instead of right at the double doors for the Red Brick, and don't rush past the waterfall at the very start — that's the cache most players miss entirely.

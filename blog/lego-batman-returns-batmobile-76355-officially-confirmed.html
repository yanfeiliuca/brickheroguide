LEGO Batman Returns Batmobile (76355) Is Officially Confirmed: Real Price, Real Dates, and No In-Game Code

📅 August 28, 2026  |  ⏱ 5 min read

Back in June and July, this site tracked a rumor working its way through the LEGO leak community: a set called 76355, allegedly recreating the 1992 Batmobile from Batman Returns, landing sometime around September 1 for somewhere between $220 and $230. On August 18, the rumor became a press release. The LEGO Group and Warner Bros. Discovery Global Consumer Products officially unveiled the LEGO® DC Batman™: Batman Returns™ Batmobile™ (76355), and the real specs are now locked in.

What's new here versus our earlier coverage: our May/June rumor breakdown and July price-update articles were both built entirely on unconfirmed leaks from Reddit's r/Legoleak. Everything below comes from LEGO's own August 18 press release, corroborated independently by The Brick Fan the same day.

The Official Numbers

Per LEGO's press release, the set is 2,269 pieces and retails for $229.99 / £189.99 / €209.99. It will be available for LEGO Insiders® Early Access starting September 1, 2026, with general availability following on September 4, 2026, through LEGO.com and LEGO Stores. The set carries an 18+ age rating, placing it in LEGO's adult collector display-model line rather than the general-audience Batmobile sets tied to the video game.

The piece count and rounded price the rumor mill landed on in July turned out to be accurate. What it got wrong was the minifigure guess — several fan sites speculated a Penguin figure would headline the set given the film's plot, but the confirmed release includes only an exclusive Batman minifigure, not a Penguin variant.

The Batmissile Transformation and Hidden Easter Eggs

The headline feature is a working transformation function: pressing a button releases the vehicle's side panels to reveal the Batmissile, the compact escape craft from the 1992 film, using what LEGO describes as an "innovative lift-arm mechanism." LEGO says this is the first time a LEGO Batmobile model has included a physical transformation feature of this kind. To build it, LEGO's design team created three new mould pieces specifically for this set, including a new Batman minifigure cowl and a newly moulded cape.

The set also includes six hidden Easter eggs referencing the film, one of which LEGO specifically calls out: a small recreation of the Penguin's tracking device from the movie, tucked into the build rather than represented as a minifigure.

No, This Doesn't Unlock Anything in Legacy of the Dark Knight

Because this site already covers the four LEGO Batman sets that do carry QR redemption codes for Legacy of the Dark Knight — the Batman Logo set (76330) and the three Batmobile sets (76331–76333) — it's worth being direct about this: 76355 is not one of them. LEGO's own official redemption page for the game lists only 76330, 76331, 76332, and 76333 as eligible for in-game golden unlocks, and 76355 does not appear anywhere on it. This is a standalone display model in LEGO's adult collector Batman line, sold independently of the video game's cross-promotion program. If you're buying it hoping for a code to redeem in-game, there isn't one.

Why the confusion is understandable: LEGO has released several Batman vehicle sets alongside Legacy of the Dark Knight this year, and four of them do carry redeemable codes. 76355 shares the same general Batman-vehicle category and roughly the same release window as the game's Switch 2 launch and Mayhem Collection DLC (both September 18), but it is a separate product line with no connection to the game's redemption system.

Bottom Line

The rumor mill's piece count and price estimate held up almost exactly. What's actually new as of this official announcement is the working Batmissile transformation, the three newly moulded parts, the confirmed two-stage release (Insiders Early Access September 1, general sale September 4), and the fact that this is a pure collector's set with no tie-in to Legacy of the Dark Knight's in-game rewards. For sets that do unlock something in-game, see our Suits & Abilities Guide.

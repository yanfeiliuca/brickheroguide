Update 1.010: WB Games Confirms 12 Mayhem Collection Trophies, Matching August's Leak

Published: September 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Two days after the Mayhem Collection DLC and the Switch 2 version both launched, Warner Bros. Games has published an official changelog for what shipped alongside them. The September 2026 Update page on LEGO Games Support confirms a detail that had been sitting unresolved on this site's own Trophy Guide since launch day: the Mayhem Collection has its own trophy list, and it's exactly the size a two-month-old leak said it would be.

The Leaked List Was Right

Back in late July, a Steam achievement list for the then-unannounced DLC leaked online, showing exactly 12 achievements — things like "Well Equipped" (buy every item from a Joker-Mite shop) and "Absolute Mayhem" (complete five Mayhem Runs). We covered that leak in detail at the time, and both our Mayhem Collection DLC guide and Trophy Guide have carried a caveat ever since: TT Games hadn't officially confirmed a PS5/Xbox trophy list existed at all, let alone that it would match the leaked Steam count.

WB Games' official September update page closes that gap. It states plainly: "12 new trophies/achievements, unlockable within the Mayhem Collection DLC on PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, and PC (Steam & Epic Games Store)." That's an exact match for the number that leaked in July — a rare case of an unofficial Steam datamine holding up perfectly against the eventual official count.

What's still not confirmed: the official update page gives a total count, not an itemized list with trophy names or PS5 rarity tiers (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum breakdown). The 12 names from the July Steam leak remain our best guide to what they likely are, but treat individual trophy names as unofficial until a platform-specific list is confirmed on PSNProfiles or TrueAchievements.

What Else Is in the September Update

Per the official changelog, alongside the trophy count, the September update covers ground this site has already reported piece by piece since launch day: the Nintendo Switch 2 version is now live, the Mayhem Collection DLC is available to Deluxe Edition owners across Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, and the free Absolute difficulty level — the single-life, no-prompts mode TT Games detailed at Gamescom — is live for every owner regardless of DLC ownership. The update also lists "additional optimization updates, performance improvements, and general quality of life fixes" without itemizing them.

That last line is worth flagging for anyone tracking specific bugs. It's the same pattern this site noted with July's Update 1.008: a real patch ships, but the notes describe it in the broadest possible terms rather than listing individual fixes. If you're waiting on a specific known issue — the WayneTech chip discrepancy after mission-skips, the Diamond Brutal Bat trophy bug, or a crash-related save loss — nothing in this update's notes confirms any of those by name, so don't assume they're resolved just because a new build landed.

The Build Landed a Day Early

According to MP1st, the underlying build — Update 1.010 (1.010.000) — was already live on September 16, two days before the Mayhem Collection unlocked on September 18. That's a normal pattern for DLC launches: the download and its assets ship ahead of time so the content can unlock server-side on the actual release date, rather than requiring a same-day multi-gigabyte download for everyone at once. MP1st's report from that pre-load window also re-confirmed the Sinister Pack's 7-suit, 1-Batmobile, 5-Batcave-item breakdown — contents first detailed back at Gamescom in August and already reflected in our DLC guide — directly from the update's own data files, two days ahead of the DLC actually unlocking.

Bottom Line

If you already own the Deluxe Edition on any platform, the Mayhem Collection and its 12 trophies are live right now — no separate download or unlock step needed beyond being on Update 1.010, which should have installed automatically. Standard Edition owners still need the $24.99 / £21.99 / €24.99 Deluxe Edition Upgrade to access the DLC and its trophy list. For anyone planning a trophy-hunting run through the new content, we'd suggest waiting a few more days for a community-verified, platform-specific trophy list with exact unlock conditions before treating any leaked name as gospel — we'll update the Trophy Guide the moment one is confirmed.

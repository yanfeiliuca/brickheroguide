Gamescom 2026 Is Two Days Away: Your LEGO Batman Watch Guide for August 25–26

📅 August 24, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

Nothing new has leaked about the Mayhem Collection since our August 20 report on the trailer and designer interview — and that's the point of this post. With Gamescom 2026 now two days out, this is a straightforward, no-speculation watch guide: what's actually happening, when it happens in your timezone, and where the DLC's first new footage since May will actually air.

The Gamescom Calendar, Confirmed

Gamescom 2026 runs August 26–30 at Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany. Ahead of the show floor opening, the general Opening Night Live showcase airs the evening before, on August 25 — that broadcast is not LEGO Batman-specific and there's no indication it will feature the game, so don't tune in expecting a Batman moment there. The date that matters for this site is August 26, when Brick Fanatics reports the Mayhem Collection's first new trailer since launch will premiere exclusively on IGN's Gamescom Studio livestream, alongside a designer interview.

Date What's Happening
August 25, 2026 Gamescom Opening Night Live showcase (general games industry event, not confirmed to feature LEGO Batman)
August 26, 2026 Gamescom show floor opens in Cologne; IGN's Gamescom Studio airs the Mayhem Collection trailer and a Tim Spence / Chris Payne designer interview
August 27–30, 2026 Gamescom 2026 public days; LEGO booth open on the show floor (playable Mayhem Collection demo not yet confirmed)
September 18, 2026 Mayhem Collection DLC releases on all platforms, alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 launch of the base game

Exactly When to Tune In on August 26

Per Brick Fanatics' report of IGN's confirmed schedule, the Mayhem Collection segment airs close to these times:

Region Time (August 26)
Pacific (PT) Around 6:00 AM
Eastern (ET) Around 9:00 AM
UK (BST) Around 2:00 PM
Central Europe (CEST) Around 3:00 PM
Set a reminder, not an alarm: WB Games and IGN haven't published an exact minute-by-minute rundown of the Gamescom Studio broadcast, so these times mark when the segment is expected within the stream — not a guaranteed start-to-finish slot. Your safest bet is to have the stream up 15–20 minutes early.

What We're Actually Expecting to See

The trailer and interview are billed as going beyond what's already public. Here's the baseline — already confirmed via WB Games' Deluxe Edition materials, not new for this post — that any August 26 reveal will be building on:

  • Mayhem Mode — The Joker and Harley Quinn become fully playable, each with unique abilities, gadgets, and takedowns
  • A new story mission — an Arkham Asylum breakout that spills into Gotham City
  • The Sinister Pack — one new suit for each of the game's 7 existing playable characters, 5 new Batcave decorations, and 1 new Batmobile skin

What's genuinely unknown — and what the designer interview is best positioned to clarify — is how Mayhem Mode is structured as a mode (a standalone sandbox versus missions woven into the main map), whether the Arkham Asylum mission connects to the base game's story chapters or sits apart from them, and whether the Mayhem Collection will be playable on Gamescom's show floor at all. WB Games' August 19 teaser didn't confirm hands-on demos, only video content, per Brick Fanatics.

Three Questions Worth Watching For

  1. Is there a release trailer, or just a teaser? A full gameplay trailer would be the strongest signal that TT Games considers the DLC feature-complete less than a month from launch.
  2. Does the interview name a price for anything beyond the existing $24.99 Deluxe Edition upgrade? That price has been confirmed since the DLC's original announcement and hasn't changed.
  3. Any hint at the "unconfirmed extras" from the June datamine? Our Mayhem Collection DLC guide tracks which datamined elements are still unofficial — Gamescom is a natural moment for WB Games to confirm or deny them.
Catching up on the DLC itself? See our August 20 announcement breakdown for the full confirmed contents and sourcing, or the Mayhem Collection DLC guide for the standing reference page we'll update once the trailer airs.

Bottom line: two days out, nothing about the Mayhem Collection's contents has changed since our last report — what's changed is that the reveal now has a hard date and time attached. We'll update BrickHeroGuide with the actual trailer and interview takeaways as soon as IGN's Gamescom Studio broadcast airs on August 26.

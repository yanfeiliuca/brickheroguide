All Vehicles in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight — Complete Roster Guide

Published: July 28, 2026 · 7 min read

Seven playable characters in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight each get their own dedicated fleet, and between story unlocks, Batcave Garage purchases, and Deluxe Edition exclusives, Gotham's roads and skies get crowded fast. We pulled together every vehicle that's been documented across the game's community guides to build a single reference for who drives what — and cleared up a real discrepancy in how many vehicles actually exist along the way.

How Many Vehicles Are There? The Count Depends on Edition

Community guides don't fully agree on a single number, and it's worth explaining why rather than picking one figure and hoping it's right. GamingBolt's vehicle guide counts 33 total vehicles across the seven playable characters, explicitly including three vehicles that are exclusive to the Deluxe Edition. TheGamer's separate vehicle guide lists 29 vehicles spread across the same seven characters, which lines up with the base-game roster once the Deluxe-only additions are set aside. Neither figure is wrong — they're answering slightly different questions, and the gap is almost entirely explained by which edition of the game you own.

The Seven-Character Vehicle Roster

Every playable character can only drive vehicles built specifically for them — only Batman can take the Batmobile out, only Catwoman can drive the Kitty Car, and so on. Across both community guides, the character-by-character breakdown is consistent:

  • Batman has by far the largest fleet, including the Batpod, Batcycle, Tumbler, and multiple Batmobile variants pulled from different eras of Batman media (including a Batman '66-inspired car and a Batman: The Animated Series-inspired Batmobile).
  • Jim Gordon drives GCPD-branded vehicles: a GCPD Cruiser, a GCPD Motorcycle, and a SWAT Van.
  • Catwoman gets the Catmobile, the Kitty Car, and a dedicated motorcycle.
  • Robin has Redbird, the Robincycle, and the Robinmobile.
  • Batgirl drives a Scooter, Batgirl's Van, and her own Batman '66-styled car.
  • Nightwing gets Nightbird, the Nightcycle, and the Wingcycle.
  • Talia al Ghul has her own motorcycle, a Sports Car, and a Talia-specific Tumbler.

A handful of vehicles per character are available immediately by default, while the rest are earned through a mix of story chapter progress, side-quest completion (including AR Trials and WayneTech Cache unlock milestones), and straight Stud purchases from the Garage inside the Batcave. Exact Stud prices for individual vehicles vary between community guides in a few spots, so rather than repeat figures that don't fully agree, we'd point you to the Batcave Garage menu in-game for the current, authoritative price on any specific vehicle you're chasing.

The Deluxe Edition-Exclusive Vehicles

GamingBolt's guide names three vehicles as Deluxe Edition-only: Batman Beyond, Batman: Arkham Trilogy, and Batmobeast, all part of Batman's fleet specifically. That lines up with how Steam's own store listing describes the Deluxe Edition's Legacy Collection, which bundles "over 30 launch-day items included across 3 Themed Packs: Arkham Trilogy Pack, Batman Beyond Pack, Party Music Pack" — and specifies that each of those three Themed Packs includes 7 suits (one per playable character), one Batmobile, and a set of five Batcave customization props. The Batmobile included in each Themed Pack is almost certainly the source of those three Deluxe-exclusive vehicles, though TT Games' own materials don't spell out that one-to-one mapping by name, so treat that connection as a reasonable inference from the official pack contents rather than a confirmed line-item.

Owning the base game alone does not grant access to these three; they require the Deluxe Edition Upgrade, which is also sold separately for $24.99 and includes the Legacy Collection alongside the upcoming Mayhem Collection DLC.

Real-World LEGO Sets Unlock Gold Vehicle Variants

Three of the physical LEGO Batmobile sets released alongside the game — the Batman v Superman Batmobile (76331), The Batman Batmobile (76332), and the Batman & Robin Batmobile (76333) — each carry a QR code that unlocks a gold-painted variant of that specific Batmobile in-game. As covered in our LEGO sets gold unlocks guide, these codes only pay off after you've already unlocked the standard black version of the matching Batmobile through in-game side quests; buying the set just gets you the code; earning the vehicle in Gotham first is still required before the gold paint shows up.

Where to Find Your Fleet

Most of the roster lives in the Garage inside the Batcave, where unlocked vehicles can be selected and purchasable ones bought with Studs once their prerequisites (chapter progress, side-quest counts, or WayneTech Cache totals) are met. For the WayneTech Caches specifically that gate several vehicles across Batgirl, Nightwing, and Talia's rosters, our WayneTech Upgrades Guide covers the full 200-cache collectible set and its unlock milestones, and our Collectibles Guide rounds up everything else worth tracking down across Gotham's four islands.

The "Epic Games Store Pending" Note Just Disappeared From LEGO Batman Legacy's Patch Page

Published: July 29, 2026 · 6 min read · Patch Watch

Five days ago, we reported that Epic Games Store owners of LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight were two patches behind, based on a single line at the bottom of WB Games' own support page: "July 2026 Update will be available for Epic Games Store later this month." We checked that same page again today, and that sentence is gone. Epic Games Store is now listed right alongside PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam — no footnote, no caveat. Here's exactly what changed, what didn't, and where the picture is still incomplete.

What we verified: We fetched WB Games' official LEGO Games Support "July 2026 Update" article directly today, July 29, 2026, and compared its exact wording against the version we quoted in our July 24 article (also a direct fetch, made that same day). We also fetched TT Games' separate news post covering the same update. Both fetches are cited below. We did not find an independent player report on Steam Community, Reddit, or elsewhere confirming the Epic Games Store build itself has updated — that distinction matters, and we spell out why below.

What Changed Between July 24 and July 29

Our July 24 article quoted WB's support page listing fixes under the heading "PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam," followed by a standalone footnote confirming Epic Games Store hadn't received any of it yet. Today, that same URL — unchanged, same article ID — presents the fix list under a single combined heading: "PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, PC (Steam & Epic Games Store)." The "later this month" sentence is no longer on the page at all.

Date Fetched Platform Heading on WB Support Page EGS Footnote Present?
July 24, 2026 "PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC" (Steam implied, EGS separate) Yes — "available for Epic Games Store later this month"
July 29, 2026 (today) "PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, PC (Steam & Epic Games Store)" No — footnote removed

The fix list itself — all ten items, from the Batpod camera issue to the Tricorner Island WayneTech Cache Chest bug we detailed last week — is identical in both versions. Nothing was added or removed from the actual changelog; only the platform framing changed.

The Catch: TT Games' Own News Page Disagrees

Here's where it gets messier. WB Games and developer TT Games run two separate official pages covering the same update — the LEGO Games Support article we've been tracking, and a news post on ttgames.com. We fetched the TT Games version today as well, and it still shows the old framing: "PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, PC (Steam*)" with an asterisked footnote reading "July 2026 Update will be available for Epic Games Store later this month." That page is dated July 15, 2026, and shows no sign of being touched since.

So as of today, the two official sources actively contradict each other: the support-ticket-facing page says Epic Games Store is caught up, and the press-facing news page says it's still waiting. We don't know which one reflects reality more accurately, and we're not going to guess which team forgot to update their copy. What we can say is that this is a genuine, verifiable discrepancy between two WB/TT Games-controlled URLs, not something we're inferring from silence.

What This Means (and What We're Not Claiming)

The most likely explanation is the boring one: the support page's wording was updated because the Epic Games Store build genuinely caught up sometime in the last five days, and whoever maintains the TT Games news post simply hasn't gotten around to syncing it. That would match the pattern from earlier in the month, where the support page consistently led on rollout status. But we want to be precise about the limits of what a wording change on a help-center article actually proves. It is not the same as an Epic Games Launcher changelog, a version number bump visible to players, or a forum post from an Epic Games Store owner saying the download landed. In our search today, we didn't find any of those three things. If you own the game on Epic Games Store, the page-level evidence points toward you being caught up — but treat it as "probably resolved" rather than "confirmed resolved" until you see the download yourself.

What Epic Games Store Owners Should Do Right Now

  • Open the Epic Games Launcher and check for a pending update on LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight directly — that's the only confirmation that actually matters for your save file.
  • If it has updated, the fixes you're receiving include the Tricorner Island WayneTech Cache Chest bug (LBAT-446) and the Batcave invisible-vehicles bug (LBAT-602) we flagged as progression-relevant in our earlier coverage — worth re-checking those specific spots if you'd previously worked around them.
  • If it hasn't updated yet, nothing about today's finding changes our earlier advice: avoid relying on Batcave vehicle displays and don't burn your only WayneTech Cache attempt on Tricorner Island until you've confirmed the patch is live on your build.
  • We'll revise this article if we find a firmer confirmation — a launcher screenshot, an updated TT Games news post, or WB explicitly stating a build number for the Epic Games Store release.

Either way, the fact that WB's own support copy moved from "later this month" to "already covered" in the space of five days — with seven days still left in July at the time of writing — is itself the story worth logging, regardless of which of the two official pages turns out to be the stale one.

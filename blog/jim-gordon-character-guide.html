Jim Gordon in LEGO Batman Legacy: Unlock, Gadgets & Why He's the Roster's Most Overlooked Pick

Published: August 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Every character guide on this site so far has covered a fan favorite — Batgirl, Nightwing, Catwoman, Talia al Ghul. Jim Gordon rarely gets the same attention, even though he's the very first ally added to your roster in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight and the character most players blow straight past on their way to unlocking someone flashier. That's a mistake early on, since his Foam Sprayer is the game's answer to several puzzle types you'll keep running into. This guide covers exactly when Gordon joins your lineup, what his two gadgets actually do, how his in-game design ties back to a specific Batman film, and where he lands on the roster's unofficial tier list.

How to Unlock Jim Gordon

Gordon becomes playable in Chapter 1, almost as soon as the story gets moving. According to Dexerto's character wiki, he unlocks after you complete the Docks mission, which lines up with GameRant's full-roster breakdown listing his unlock timing simply as "Chapter 1" — the earliest of any Bat-Family ally, with only Batman himself available sooner. In the story, this is the point where Bruce Wayne has just set out as Batman for the first time and Gordon, still working his way up through the GCPD toward the police commissioner role he holds in the wider Batman mythos, starts feeding intel and backup to the new vigilante.

Tip: Because Gordon unlocks so early, don't skip switching to him during Chapter 1 side content — some early WayneTech puzzles are built around his Foam Sprayer, and you'll otherwise have to backtrack once you realize you're missing the right tool.

Jim Gordon's Gadgets & Abilities

Gordon's kit is the least acrobatic on the roster, and that's by design — he's a cop, not a martial artist. GameRant's gadget breakdown lists two tools:

💧 Foam Sprayer

Puzzle-Solving + Crowd Control

Fires sticky foam that gums up machinery and pipes, and temporarily incapacitates enemies caught in the blast. It's also the gadget used to freeze spinning cogs — a mechanism you'll see gating off collectibles and side paths across Gotham, per GameRant's exploration breakdown.

🎯 Rebound Launcher

Ranged Combat

Fires a shot that bounces off walls and surfaces before hitting its target, letting Gordon smash objects or damage multiple enemies in one shot rather than aiming directly at each one.

Where Jim Gordon Ranks on the Roster

Dexerto's character tier list places Gordon alone in C-Tier — the only roster member not sharing his tier with anyone else, sitting below Catwoman and Robin in B-Tier, Batgirl and Nightwing in A-Tier, and Batman and Talia al Ghul at the top in S-Tier. It's worth noting this is Dexerto's own editorial ranking rather than an official developer statement, and it reflects combat versatility more than puzzle utility — Gordon's Foam Sprayer stays relevant for mechanism-based content well after you've unlocked flashier characters, even if he's not who you'd pick for a tough fight.

Jim Gordon's Design & Suits

Gordon's default in-game look draws from a specific source: according to TheDirect's rundown of the game's DC character roster, his design is based on the version of Jim Gordon who appears in 2022's The Batman. The same report notes that his unlockable suit variants range much further than that single film, spanning looks inspired by Batman: The Animated Series through to the gritty detective aesthetic of the 2022 film, each with its own distinct look. That range fits the site's broader suits count — the game ships with 101 base costumes across the full cast, rising to 129 once all DLC is included.

Practical Tips for Using Jim Gordon

  • Keep him equipped for cog puzzles. Any spinning-cog mechanism blocking a path or collectible is almost always a Foam Sprayer job — don't burn time trying to solve it with another character.
  • Use the Rebound Launcher in tight rooms. Its bounce mechanic is most useful against grouped enemies or breakable objects in enclosed spaces, where a bounced shot is more likely to connect with something.
  • Don't skip him once flashier characters unlock. His gadgets don't become obsolete — several environmental puzzles later in the game still require the Foam Sprayer specifically.
  • Pair him with a melee-focused character in co-op. Gordon's kit leans support and crowd control, so a partner who can close distance covers what he can't.
Full roster context: Gordon joins a seven-character playable lineup that also includes Batman, Catwoman, Robin, Batgirl, Nightwing, and Talia Al Ghul. For where each character unlocks, see our All Characters & Unlocks Guide, and for the roster's top-ranked tech pick, check our Batgirl Character Guide.

Verdict: Is Jim Gordon Worth Using?

Jim Gordon isn't going to top anyone's favorite-character list, and Dexerto's tier list backs that up by ranking him last on the roster. But dismissing him after Chapter 1 is a mistake — the Foam Sprayer keeps mattering for cog-based puzzles well into the game, and he's the only character with that specific tool. Treat him as a utility pick you swap to for specific mechanism puzzles rather than someone you main through combat, and he pulls his weight.

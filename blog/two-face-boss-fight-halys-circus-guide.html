Two-Face Boss Fight: The Complete Haly's Circus Strategy & Collectibles Guide

📅 August 26, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

Two-Face's boss fight sits inside Mission 10: Two-Face, the "Haly's Circus" level that closes out the early half of Chapter 3. It's an unusual encounter by this game's standards — roughly half the mission is a parkour and bomb-defusal gauntlet strung between circus set pieces, and the actual coin-flip boss fight against Two-Face himself is threaded through it rather than being one isolated arena. It's also a milestone mission: this is where Dick Grayson is introduced and becomes playable as Robin for the rest of the game. This guide covers the fight's three phases, the two-round bomb-defusal parkour that surrounds it, and where to find all 5 Coins and the Red Brick: Clown.

Required characters: Batman and Robin. You'll also briefly control Bruce Wayne (out of costume) for a few circus set-piece builds before the fight proper begins — that's normal, not a bug.

Robin's Debut: The Birdarang and Cable Launcher

The mission opens with Dick Grayson performing as part of the Flying Graysons at Haly's Circus, and you'll spend the first stretch alternating between Bruce Wayne (building spotlight rigs and platforms) and Dick as Robin, who introduces his Birdarang here — functionally similar to Batman's Batarang, but able to highlight and hit three targets in a single throw. This section also teaches the Cable Launcher, which you'll use constantly for the rest of the mission to cross gaps, build zip-line rope bridges, and reach the trapeze. Once the pair reach a wrapped present at the end of the acrobatics sequence, Batman and Robin officially meet, and lighting a cannon fuse triggers the fight that brings Two-Face into the level.

The Three-Phase Coin-Flip Fight

Two-Face's actual combat encounter is built around his signature coin flip, and the animation tells you exactly what's coming next:

PhaseTriggerWhat to Do
Phase 1 — HeadsCoin lands heads; Two-Face charges in for a melee rush.Sidestep the charge and counter immediately with Robin's bo-staff combo — it hits both sides and drops him faster than Batman's grapple setup.
Phase 2 — TailsCoin lands tails; Two-Face retreats to the trapeze rigging.He calls in henchmen and lobs grenades from above. Clear enemies between his rushes so you don't get sandwiched, and use Robin's ranged throw on the strength-test targets to drop platforms and cut off his position.
Phase 3 — CombinedTwo-Face drops to low health.He starts mixing the melee charge and ranged grenades in the same phase. Trigger the four corner rigging traps when he stops to flip the coin — it strips roughly half his remaining health at once — then finish him with Robin's pole-vault attack off the trapeze.

None of the three phases require anything beyond Batman and Robin, and the henchmen he summons in Phase 2 are the main threat to watch — they're what turns an otherwise readable fight into a scramble if you let them stack up while chasing Two-Face around the rigging.

The Bomb-Defusal Parkour (Two Rounds)

What makes this mission longer than most Chapter 3 encounters is that Two-Face's coin flip also gates a pair of platforming courses tied to defusing his 100-stick TNT rig. After the first cutscene where Two-Face reveals the bomb, you'll run a parkour section: jump onto the green platforms while avoiding spike traps, use a big-bounce pad to clear a higher ledge, then dodge barrels rolling down a staircase. Past the barrels you'll cross a swinging axe and balance between two spinning grinders before reaching a grapple-bar climb where a thug can be taken down from behind. From there, use the Cable Launcher to reach the bomb itself, switch to Batman, and use the Bat Claw to pop it open before a quick minigame throws the dynamite clear. A second round of enemies follows immediately after.

A second, slightly harder version of the same parkour course repeats later in the mission, ending with Robin using the trapeze to defuse the bomb for good. Treat both rounds as timing puzzles first and combat second — getting greedy chasing extra hits on henchmen mid-parkour is the most common way to eat unnecessary damage here.

All 5 Coins and the Red Brick: Clown

The mission's collectibles — 5 Coins and 1 Red Brick — are all reachable on a single playthrough with just Batman and Robin, no missable-gadget requirements:

  • Coin 1: Just before hitting the big target below the white clown, during the Cable Launcher tutorial section — look left of Bruce Wayne's starting position after crossing the rope with the swinging hammer.
  • Coin 2: Right after the first Two-Face cutscene, hanging on a red balloon to the right — shoot it down.
  • Coin 3: Immediately after the barrel-dodging section in the first bomb-defusal parkour.
  • Coin 4: On the starting platform of the second bomb-defusal parkour, before you begin the second run.
  • Coin 5: Same location as Coin 3, just after the barrels — but during the second parkour round.

The Red Brick: Clown is tied to a hammer-strength minigame you'll find between the two bomb-defusal rounds, in the section where you're fighting enemies on your way back to the bomb. Look up for a hammer/strength-test game with the Red Brick sitting on top of it, jump on the button to start the swing, then hit all three targets as Robin before the indicator drops back down.

Bottom line: This is really two mini-challenges stitched together — a three-phase coin-flip boss fight against Two-Face, and a two-round bomb-defusal parkour gauntlet around it. Learn the Heads/Tails tell, don't let henchmen stack up during Phase 2, and sweep the 5 Coins and Red Brick: Clown as you go rather than backtracking.

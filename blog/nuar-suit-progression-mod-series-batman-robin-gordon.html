One Modder Is Building a Full Era-by-Era Suit Timeline for Batman, Robin, and Gordon

📅 August 11, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

Most of the cosmetic mods on the LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight Nexus page are one-off suit swaps — a single alternate costume, uploaded once, occasionally patched. What Nexus Mods creator Nuar12z has been quietly building since late July is something more ambitious: a matching set of "suit progression" mods that reimagine each character's look across different eras of Batman lore, all in the same visual style and all clearly part of one connected project. Over the past ten days that project has gone from one character to three.

It Started With Batman, Back in July

The first entry, Nuar's Dark Knight Path (Suit Progression for Batman), originally went up on Nexus Mods on July 22, 2026. Its description frames the concept directly: "My take on Batman suits through his career in the game. Inspired by Dan Mora, Vin Lopez, DarkPrinceRevan art." Rather than a single alternate costume, the mod maps seven suits onto in-fiction "years" of Batman's career — Year Zero (a Golden Age-style suit), Year One (The Batman 2022 suit), Year Four (Brave and the Bold), Year Eleven (Batman: The Animated Series), Year Fifteen (a 1989 Tim Burton-film-inspired suit), Year Seventeen (Gotham by Gaslight), and Year Twenty (an "All in Batman" design) — with both an original and a "Remade" version of each. As of this week the mod sits at version 2.3, last updated August 10, 2026, with seven files and 57 screenshots documenting the full lineup.

Then Robin, on August 8

On August 8, Nuar12z uploaded a companion piece: Nuar's Apprentice Path (Suit Progression for Robin). Its description mirrors the Batman mod almost word for word — "My take on Robin suits through his career in the game. Inspired by Dan Mora, Vin Lopez, DarkPrinceRevan art" — confirming this is the same continuity, just extended to a second character. The Robin mod is smaller in scope, covering two suits: a "Younger Robin Suit" styled after the Batman Forever-era look, and an "Older Robin Suit" styled after the New 52 design. It was updated to version 1.2 the following day, August 9.

And Now Gordon, on August 10

The series' newest entry, Nuar's Paragon of Justice Path (Suit for Gordon), went up on August 10 — making it, as of this article, the freshest mod in the whole series. Its description reads simply: "Suit for Jim Gordon inspired by his appearance in other media," and it replaces Gordon's default Cop Suit in-game. Unlike the Batman and Robin entries, this is currently a single-suit release rather than a multi-era set, and it's brand new enough that it hadn't picked up any endorsements yet at the time of writing.

Where to find them: All three mods are published under the same Nexus Mods account, Nuar12z. Search "Nuar" from the game's mod list on Nexus Mods to find the full set in one place.

Why This Is Worth Watching

Individually, none of these three mods is the kind of headline-grabbing release that dominates a "best mods" list. What's notable is the pattern: three months after launch, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight's modding scene has moved past isolated one-off skins toward creators building out sustained, multi-part projects with a consistent creative through-line. A single author committing to matching art direction across Batman, Robin, and now Gordon — and shipping new suits or updates on a near-weekly cadence through early-to-mid August — is a different kind of community activity than the early post-launch wave of individual cosmetic swaps this site covered back in June and July.

It's also a reasonable bet that the series isn't finished. Nuar12z has now covered three named characters in six weeks, each mod explicitly framed as one character's "path" through different eras of the character's visual history. Whether the next entry covers Nightwing, Catwoman, or another of the game's seven playable characters is anyone's guess, but the release cadence so far — Batman in July, Robin on August 8, Gordon on August 10 — suggests more is likely on the way before the Mayhem Collection DLC lands on September 18.

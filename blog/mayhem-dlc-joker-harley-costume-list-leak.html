The Full Leaked Joker & Harley Quinn Costume List for the Mayhem Collection DLC

Published: July 25, 2026 · 6 min read · Rumor Watch
Rumor Warning: Everything below comes from datamined game files posted by a third-party leaker in May 2026, not from an official TT Games or WB Games announcement. None of it has been confirmed. Treat every costume name as speculative until the Mayhem Collection's official reveal.

Back in our coverage of the Task Force X datamine, we mentioned that leaked files reportedly described a "Mayhem Hideout" hub with a costume shop attached — but we didn't publish the actual costume list at the time, since it needed independent cross-checking first. Two months on, with the Mayhem Collection's September 18 release date drawing closer and two independent outlets having published the identical costume names from the same leaked source, here's the full breakdown of every Joker and Harley Quinn costume reportedly sitting in the game's files.

Confirmed vs. Leaked: Don't Mix These Up

It's worth separating two different things before the list, since they're easy to conflate. The Sinister Pack is officially confirmed by WB Games as part of the Mayhem Collection: seven suits, one for each of the base game's seven original playable characters (Batman, Robin, Nightwing, Batgirl, Catwoman, Jim Gordon, and Alfred), plus five Batcave decorations and one Batmobile skin. We cover that in our suits & abilities guide and our dedicated Mayhem Collection DLC guide. The costume list below is different: it's an unconfirmed, datamined roster of alternate skins specifically for Joker and Harley Quinn themselves, who become playable for the first time in Mayhem Mode. WB Games has not confirmed that Joker and Harley will have alternate costumes at all.

The Source: X0X_LEAK's May 2026 Datamine

The list traces back to a single source: the X/Twitter account X0X_LEAK, who posted two threads on May 24, 2026 — two days after launch — claiming to have pulled the information directly from the game's files. One thread listed the Task Force X characters we covered previously; the second listed the costume names below. That second thread was independently reported, with identical costume names, by ComicBook.com on May 24 and AllKeyShop on May 26. VICE's May 25 report on the same datamine covered the character list but did not reproduce the costume names. Neither TT Games nor WB Games has commented on the leak in the two months since.

What These Names Seem to Reference

Even without official confirmation, the naming pattern lines up cleanly with LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight's established style of pulling costumes from across eight decades of Batman media — the same approach used for the base game's 101 suits, detailed in our suits guide. Entries like B66, B89, and Beyond point to the 1966 TV series, the 1989 Tim Burton film, and Batman Beyond respectively. Dark Knight, Dark Knight Returns, and The Batman line up with Christopher Nolan's trilogy, Frank Miller's graphic novel, and Matt Reeves' 2022 film. On the Harley side, BOP, HQ Show, Rebirth, and White Knight point toward Birds of Prey, the adult-animated Harley Quinn series, DC's Rebirth comics relaunch, and the Batman: White Knight comic line. If accurate, it would make Joker and Harley's cosmetic rosters comparable in scope to the base roster's per-character suit counts, rather than a token handful of DLC skins.

Grain of Salt: Still Unconfirmed Two Months Later

A few things are worth keeping in mind before treating this as a shopping list. First, datamined files reflect what's present in the game's assets at a given point in time — not necessarily what ships. Costumes can be cut, renamed, or held back for a later season. Second, no additional datamine activity on this specific list has surfaced publicly since the original May 24 tweets; the story hasn't been updated or expanded by X0X_LEAK or any other source since. Third, and most importantly, WB Games' own confirmed Mayhem Collection details — covered in our DLC confirmation breakdown — make no mention of a Joker/Harley costume shop, alternate skins, or anything matching this list. Until that changes, this remains exactly what it's labeled as: an unconfirmed leak, not a roadmap.

We'll update our Mayhem Collection DLC guide the moment WB Games confirms or denies any of this. With September 18 now less than two months away, an official reveal trailer showing off Joker and Harley's actual moveset and wardrobe is likely to land well before then.

Mayhem Collection Trailer Breakdown: Joker & Harley's Confirmed Abilities, the Full Sinister Pack, and a New Free Difficulty

📅 August 27, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

The two previous entries on this site tracked the Mayhem Collection's Gamescom build-up as a countdown: a confirmation date, then a trailer date. That trailer premiered on August 26 during IGN's Gamescom Studio livestream, and Warner Bros. Games backed it with an official press release that finally names the mechanics rather than just teasing them. This is the first time the DLC's actual gameplay — not just its cast and release date — has been confirmed in writing.

What's new here versus our earlier coverage: our August 3 achievements leak article and the Mayhem Collection guide already covered the Joker-Mite shop, hideout expansion, and A.R.G.U.S. enemies from datamined Steam achievement text. Everything below is different: it's the first official description of how Joker and Harley actually fight, plus a full, WB-confirmed Sinister Pack list that supersedes the speculative costume leak from July.

Joker and Harley Quinn's Confirmed Combat Styles

Per Warner Bros. Games' official press release, the trailer shows each villain with a distinct combat identity rather than a reskinned Batman moveset. The Joker is built around speed and mobility, described as "fast-paced" and "agile," and wields a Paintball Pelter alongside a Boxing Glove weapon. Harley Quinn plays as a chaos-focused brawler: her kit includes her signature hammer, the ability to summon her pet hyenas Bud & Lou as combat allies, and a Fun Gun that fires Confetti and Bubblegum rounds instead of bullets. It's a clean split — Joker for hit-and-run mobility, Harley for area chaos and summons — and it's the first time TT Games has handed players full control of any of Gotham's rogues gallery since the base game launched in May.

New Story Mission and Mayhem Mode

The DLC's new story content is a single mission where players control both villains as they "orchestrate a breakout from Arkham Asylum with a going-away party Gotham City won't soon forget" — the Arkham Asylum Jailbreak mission this site first confirmed back in June. Beyond that mission, Mayhem Mode is a separate, repeatable activity: time-limited Mayhem Runs where players smash landmarks, stores, and vehicles across Gotham for studs while racing back to Amusement Mile before a clock runs out, with Batman and Robin in active pursuit. Enemies during these runs are A.R.G.U.S. Agents and members of Amanda Waller's Task Force X, specifically named as Deathstroke, Killer Croc, Captain Boomerang, Deadshot, King Shark, and Polka-Dot Man. This lines up with the "Absolute Mayhem" achievement (complete 5 Mayhem Runs) from the July leak, confirming the community's run-based structure theory was correct.

The Sinister Pack: Full Official Suit List

This replaces the unconfirmed May 2026 datamine list this site covered in its costume leak article, which was about Joker and Harley's own alternate suits. The official Sinister Pack instead gives every existing playable character in the base game a Joker-themed outfit, each tied to a specific real DC comic issue:

Alongside the seven suits, the pack adds a Jokermobile vehicle (based on the Arkham trilogy design) and five Batcave customization items: a Helmet based on The Merciless, a Joker Fish Aquarium, a Barbatos Statue, Snake Clowns, and a Long Halloween Pumpkin. Separately, Joker and Harley themselves will have unlockable outfits referencing other Batman media, including The Dark Knight Returns comics and Batman: The Animated Series — exact designs for those weren't shown in this trailer.

A Free Difficulty Upgrade for Everyone

The one piece of news here that isn't DLC-locked: an "Absolute" difficulty setting arrives September 18 as a free update for every owner of LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, DLC or not. WB Games describes it as harder than both existing Caped Crusader and Dark Knight difficulties, demanding more precision and awareness in combat. Don't confuse this with the "Absolute Mayhem" achievement name from the July leak — that's an unrelated Mayhem Run milestone, while Absolute difficulty is a standalone combat setting.

Release and Pricing Recap

Mayhem Collection launches September 18 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam and Epic Games Store), and Nintendo Switch 2 — the same day the Switch 2 version of the base game releases. Deluxe Edition owners get it automatically; Standard Edition owners on existing platforms can upgrade for $24.99 / £21.99, which also unlocks the earlier Legacy Collection pack. None of this changes the pricing this site has tracked since the Deluxe Edition guide went live.

Bottom line: Gamescom didn't just confirm Joker and Harley are playable — it confirmed how they play, gave the Sinister Pack a full comic-accurate suit list, and added a free difficulty tier that has nothing to do with the DLC itself.

Bane: Round 2 Boss Fight Guide — LEGO Batman Legacy's Penultimate Battle

📅 August 15, 2026  |  ⏱ 7 min read

Bane shows up twice in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, and the fight players actually ask about is the second one — the Chapter 6 rematch inside the mission "The League of Shadows Returns," voiced by Matt Berry. This is the fight that closes out the League of Shadows arc before the campaign's true final confrontation, and it's noticeably longer and more mechanically dense than his first appearance. This guide covers both rounds of that Chapter 6 encounter phase by phase, the collectibles you'll pass on the way to Wayne Tower, and what the fight sets up immediately afterward.

Not the final boss: If you're looking for the campaign's actual last fight, that's Talia al Ghul in the following mission, "The Dark Knight Returns," fought on top of Wayne Tower. Bane's Chapter 6 rematch is the penultimate battle — the one standing between you and it.

Getting to the Fight: Wayne Tower Plaza

"The League of Shadows Returns" drops you into a Gotham on lockdown — Batmobiles patrolling, drones scanning for vigilantes, and League of Shadows goons stationed around a route that funnels toward Wayne Tower. Along the way you'll need a Hackarang thrown at League tumbler terminals to disable their guns before they kill you at close range; ignoring a tumbler and walking up to it is one of the more common early deaths on this mission. Once you clear the street-level gauntlet and cross the subway tunnel, the path opens onto the plaza where Bane is waiting for the rematch.

Bane: Round 2 — Phase Breakdown

Unlike his shorter first appearance, this fight runs across two full rounds with a mid-fight power-up. Treat it as a sequence of readable attack windows rather than a straight damage race — several of Bane's moves are designed to be punished only at specific moments.

StageWhat HappensHow to Handle It
Round 1 openingBane straight-line charges repeatedly across the plaza.Keep your distance during the charge, then batarang-stun him the moment it ends.
Crate throwsBane grabs crates from the arena and winds up to throw them.Keep hitting him during the windup to cancel the throw before it lands.
Kneel windowAfter three canceled throws, Bane drops to one knee.This is your biggest damage window in Round 1 — burst his health while he's kneeling.
Round 2, Phase 1A fresh straight-line charge, now paired with a jumping shockwave and thrown bombs.Dodge the charge and punish with melee or batarang; jump over the shockwave; catch and punch bombs back at him.
Round 2, Phase 2 (Venom-boosted)Bane pumps Venom and unleashes a vacuum spin attack that pulls you in.Roll outward, away from the suck-in, rather than trying to out-run it in a straight line.
Post-spin stunBane is briefly weakened right after the spin ends.Grapple his back to extend the stun and land a bigger burst of damage.
Henchmen waveBane jumps out of the arena and sends in League henchmen while he's gone.Clear them steadily; he returns once the wave is dealt with.
Final hurricane attackBane's last attack is a shockwave-slam combo.Jump each slam individually, then pull the mechanism from his back to finish him.

Required Characters

You'll need Batgirl and Nightwing active for this mission — both are used for terminal hacks and traversal on the way to the plaza, and their gadgets factor into clearing the tumblers and reaching the fight itself. Bring whichever melee-focused third character you're comfortable with for the actual Bane exchanges; the fight doesn't gate progress behind a specific ability beyond Batgirl's and Nightwing's kits.

Collectibles on the Way to Wayne Tower

This mission carries five League Banners, five WayneTech Caches, and one Red Brick, all found before you reach Bane — there's nothing to collect in the boss arena itself, so sweep the route on your way in rather than trying to backtrack.

  • League Banners: one above an archway on the lower street at the start, one past the bollards up a staircase at the T-junction, one in the subway tunnel across from a beam, one visible partway across the subway zipline, and the last hanging between two buildings just before Wayne Tower.
  • WayneTech Caches: one beside a derelict building on the lower street, one on a grapple-accessible platform above the shutters on the main street, one along the water inside the subway tunnel, one that spills out of a car after Nightwing tethers its boot to the wall, and the last hidden under the freeway near a League tumbler — hackarang the tumbler's terminal first or it will kill you at range.
  • Red Brick (Bats color modifier): after exiting the subway, head away from the ladder toward the tumblers to find a garbage truck. Have Nightwing charge it with his battle staff to open a terminal, throw a hackarang at it, and the truck rolls forward to shield you while you grab the brick.

What Happens After Bane Falls

Beating this fight doesn't end the mission with a clean victory screen — the story rolls straight into the next confrontation. With the League of Shadows arc closed out, the campaign moves directly into "The Dark Knight Returns," the true final mission, fought against Talia al Ghul on top of Wayne Tower with Batman, Batgirl, and Nightwing all in play. If you want the full rundown of that fight instead of spoilers here, see our Chapter walkthrough hub and All 10 Boss Fights roundup for the complete campaign boss order.

Bottom line: Round 1 is about punishing crate-throw windups and the kneel state; Round 2 is about respecting the Venom spin and using the post-spin stun for your biggest damage burst. Clear the tumblers on your way in so you're not fighting the plaza and Bane's rematch at the same time.

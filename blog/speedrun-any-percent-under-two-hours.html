Speedrun Watch: redniko Breaks the 2-Hour Barrier in LEGO Batman Legacy's Any%

Published: August 5, 2026 · 6 min read

The Any% leaderboard for LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Speedrun.com has a new top time, and it's a meaningful jump: runner redniko now holds first place with a 2 hour 00 minute 04 second full-game clear, verified about a month ago. That's the first sub-2:10 time on the board, and it caps off a run of leaderboard movement that's gone largely unreported on the site until now. Here's exactly how the standings have shifted, sourced directly from the game's official Speedrun.com leaderboard and stats pages.

The Current Any% (Full Game) Leaderboard

The Any% category tracked here is Solo, PC — the leaderboard's default filter and the one with the most submitted runs. As of this writing, the top six standings are:

kwazrr's 2h 29m 01s run was the headline time when the category was still young, but it's since been passed twice — first by Siedemnastek, then by redniko, who currently sits alone under the 2-hour-10-minute mark. One technical note worth flagging for accuracy: the top five times on this board use Loading Removed Time (LRT), which strips out load-screen duration, while xioni15's sixth-place run is timed under Real Time Attempt (RTA) rules, which counts loads. The two timing methods aren't directly comparable second-for-second, so treat the sixth-place gap as somewhat larger than the raw numbers suggest.

Individual Level Records Are Still Falling

The full-game board isn't the only place seeing fresh times. Two individual-level (IL) console records were posted in the past three weeks: DMsSpeedruns set the current best for the Arkham Asylum level at 8 minutes 1 second (15 days ago), and gordosgames21 took the top spot on The League of Shadows at 13 minutes 57 seconds (17 days ago) — both under the Story Solo, Console(RTA) category. Separately, Siedemnastek posted a Replay Story category best of 1 hour 11 minutes 54 seconds on PC (21 days ago), a different category from the Any% board above that covers replaying story missions from a save file rather than a fresh-file run. And redniko, already the current Any% leader, also holds multiple PC-category IL bests set roughly a month ago, including 5 minutes 16 seconds on The League of Shadows Returns and 3 minutes 9 seconds on Tumbler Chase.

Worth noting: Console-category IL leaderboards for this game are still thin compared to the PC boards, so a "first place" console time doesn't necessarily mean it beat a large field of competitors — it may simply be among the first submissions in that specific category. That doesn't make the times less real, but it's a different kind of achievement than displacing an established PC record.

Community Growth Snapshot

Pulling from the live Speedrun.com stats page, the community now stands at 72 total runs (24 full-game, 48 individual-level), submitted by 17 total players, all currently listed as active. The page has 86 followers, and the combined total run time across every submitted run is 2 days, 21 hours, 57 minutes, and 47 seconds. The Challenge category remains empty with zero submitted runs, consistent with what this site has reported previously — challenge-mode routing tends to develop later, after runners have largely settled the more straightforward Any% and IL categories.

What Could Shake Up the Boards Next

The next real inflection point for this leaderboard is still the Mayhem Collection DLC, arriving September 18, 2026, with Joker and Harley Quinn as new playable characters and a new Arkham Asylum story mission. New playable characters and a new mission typically mean new routing questions and, in many cases, entirely new categories to compete in — the kind of open leaderboard that let redniko, Siedemnastek, and kwazrr establish themselves as the game's top Any% runners in the weeks after launch. Runners looking to get in early on a fresh category again have about six weeks to prepare.

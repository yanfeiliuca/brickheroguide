What the $24.99 Deluxe Upgrade Actually Buys You, Per Steam's Own Listing

📅 August 12, 2026  |  ⏱ 5 min read

One question keeps coming up in the comments on our Deluxe Edition and Mayhem Collection coverage: if you buy the $24.99 Deluxe Edition Upgrade today, do you have to buy the Mayhem Collection separately when it launches on September 18? Or is it already covered? We went straight to the source — Steam's own product listing for the upgrade — and the page answers the question in its own words, in a footnote most players will never scroll down far enough to read.

The Product Page's Own Answer

The Deluxe Edition Upgrade is sold on Steam as its own listing (app ID 4468750), separate from the base game and separate from the standalone Mayhem Collection page. Its "About This Content" section describes itself as containing two things: the Legacy Collection and the Mayhem Collection. Then, directly under the Mayhem Collection bullet points, sits this asterisked note, quoted verbatim from the listing:

Steam's exact wording: "Mayhem Collection is a separate post-launch DLC releasing September 18, 2026 and will be included in the LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight Deluxe Edition Upgrade (full base game required)."

In plain terms: one $24.99 purchase today unlocks the Legacy Collection immediately and the Mayhem Collection automatically the moment it releases in September. There is no second purchase, no separate unlock code, and no additional prompt needed — the listing frames it as content that "will be included," not content you'll need to redeem again later. The one hard requirement, stated on the same page, is that the upgrade "requires the base game … in order to play" — it's an add-on, not a standalone product.

What's Actually Bundled

The listing itself breaks the two collections down like this:

Collection Status Contents (per Steam listing)
Legacy Collection Available now 3 themed packs (Arkham Trilogy, Batman Beyond, Party Music). Each pack: 7 suits (one per playable character), 1 Batmobile, 5 Batcave customization props.
Mayhem Collection Releases Sept 18, 2026 New Story Mission + Mayhem Mode (Joker & Harley Quinn playable), plus the Sinister Pack: 7 suits, 1 Batmobile, 5 Batcave props.

Readers who want the full suit-by-suit rundown of the Legacy Collection's three packs can find it in our Legacy Collection breakdown, and the complete confirmed-vs-leaked picture of the Mayhem Collection is in our Mayhem Collection guide. What the Steam listing adds that neither of those pages emphasizes is the plain confirmation that both collections ship through the exact same $24.99 purchase.

A Small But Fresh Data Point: The Upgrade Has Its Own Reviews

Because the Deluxe Edition Upgrade is listed as its own product on Steam, it also carries its own separate review score — distinct from the base game's much larger review count, which passed 13,000 back on August 10. As of this week, the upgrade listing shows two overlapping numbers worth understanding correctly: the headline badge reads "Positive (28) — 100% of the 28 user reviews for this game are positive," which counts only verified Steam purchasers. The fuller review widget below it shows a wider pool of 50 total reviews (48 positive, 2 negative — a 96% positive rate) once reviews from non-Steam purchases are included. Either way you read it, early sentiment on the upgrade itself is strongly positive, though the sample size is still small compared to the base game.

System Requirements Match the Base Game

The listing also republishes the same PC system requirements as the base game: minimum spec calls for an Intel Core i5-10600K or AMD Ryzen 5 1600, 16 GB RAM, and a GTX 960 / RX 6400 / Arc A580-class GPU with 4 GB VRAM, all under Windows 11. Recommended spec steps up to a Core i7-12700 or Ryzen 7 5800X with an RTX 2070 SUPER / RX 6650 XT / Arc B580-class GPU with 8 GB VRAM. Storage is listed at 50 GB for both tiers. That's a useful confirmation for anyone wondering whether the DLC adds extra hardware overhead on top of the base install — per Steam's own listing, it doesn't change the requirements at all.

Bottom line: If you already own the base game and are deciding whether to buy the Deluxe Edition Upgrade now versus waiting for Mayhem Collection to release standalone in September, Steam's own listing settles it — one $24.99 purchase today covers both collections automatically, no second purchase required in September.

We'll continue tracking the Mayhem Collection countdown as September 18 approaches, including whatever Gamescom Cologne (August 25–30) reveals in the meantime.

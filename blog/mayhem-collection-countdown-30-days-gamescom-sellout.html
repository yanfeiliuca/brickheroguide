Mayhem Collection Countdown: 30 Days Out, and Gamescom 2026 Just Sold Out for the First Time Ever

📅 August 19, 2026  |  ⏱ 6 min read

Ten days ago we put the Mayhem Collection at 40 days out. Today that number is 30 — and the bigger story this week isn't the DLC itself, it's the show that might reveal more about it. Gamescom 2026 just sold out every single exhibition booth for the first time in the event's history, according to Inven Global, with LEGO confirmed among the exhibitor list alongside Google, Microsoft, Nintendo, SEGA, and Capcom. The show floor opens in exactly one week. Here's the state of play.

What's Actually Confirmed for the Mayhem Collection

Nothing about the DLC's core contents has changed since Warner Bros. Games' own Deluxe Edition press materials went out in May — this is still the official breakdown, not a rumor.

🎵 Mayhem Collection — Confirmed Contents

Available September 18, 2026, included with the Deluxe Edition
  • Mayhem Mode — a new mode featuring The Joker and Harley Quinn as fully playable characters, each with their own abilities, gadgets, and takedowns
  • New Story Mission — The Joker and Harley Quinn break out of Arkham Asylum and cause chaos on the streets of Gotham City
  • Sinister Pack — 7 new suits (one per original playable character), 5 new Batcave decorations, and 1 new Batmobile skin

September 18 is a double date on the calendar: it's also when the game finally launches on Nintendo Switch 2, meaning the Mayhem Collection and the Switch 2 debut land simultaneously.

Gamescom 2026 Sells Out for the First Time Ever

This is the actual news since our last check-in. Inven Global reported on July 22 that Gamescom 2026's exhibition booths — spanning roughly 230,000 square meters — sold out completely for the first time in the show's history, a sign of record-high demand. Confirmed exhibitors now include Google, Microsoft, LEGO, SEGA, Capcom, PLAION, Sony Pictures, Tencent, Nintendo, Ubisoft, Electronic Arts, HoYoverse, Bandai Namco, and Konami, among others.

The schedule: Gamescom Dev, the developer-focused conference, opens things up on August 24. Opening Night Live, the global showcase hosted by Geoff Keighley, follows on August 25. The main show floor at Koelnmesse in Cologne runs August 26–30, with the 26th reserved for business and media attendees only before the public days begin.

What LEGO Group Still Hasn't Said

Brick Fanatics confirmed on July 20 that the LEGO Group is returning to Gamescom this year, but was explicit that the specifics of the booth haven't been detailed publicly. That matters here because last year's Gamescom is literally where LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight was first revealed to the world, as part of a larger booth that also covered Batman, One Piece, and Super Mario tie-ins.

Brick Fanatics flagged the Mayhem Collection — releasing just three weeks after the show closes — as “a likely candidate” for a fuller reveal. That's informed speculation from a LEGO-focused outlet, not confirmation from WB Games or TT Games, so treat it as a reasonable bet rather than a lock. Two things are still genuinely unknown: whether the Mayhem Collection will be sold standalone to Standard Edition owners (and at what price), and whether there's any extended Joker or Harley Quinn gameplay footage beyond the original May reveal trailer.

Countdown Timeline

New here? The Mayhem Collection is one of four content packs bundled with the Deluxe Edition — the other three (Arkham Trilogy, Batman Beyond, and Party Music) shipped at launch as the cosmetic-only Legacy Collection. See our full Legacy Collection breakdown for everything else included.

Bottom line: the Mayhem Collection's contents haven't shifted, but the runway to Gamescom just got a lot more crowded — a sold-out show floor with LEGO on the confirmed exhibitor list is exactly the kind of setup that produces a trailer drop. We'll update this page the moment anything official lands.

Mayhem Collection Countdown: 40 Days Out, and Gamescom 2026 Could Reveal More

📅 August 9, 2026  |  ⏱ 5 min read

As of today, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight's next major content drop is exactly 40 days away. The Mayhem Collection DLC is locked in for September 18, 2026 — the same day the game finally lands on Nintendo Switch 2 — and it's shaping up to be the most substantial post-launch addition TT Games has planned for the game so far. In between now and then sits Gamescom 2026 (August 26–30 in Cologne), an event with a track record for this franchise: it's literally where Legacy of the Dark Knight was first announced, one year ago. Here's what's actually confirmed for the Mayhem Collection, and why the show floor is worth watching.

What's Actually Confirmed So Far

The core details of the Mayhem Collection come straight from Warner Bros. Games' own Deluxe Edition press materials, published alongside the game's May 2026 launch trailer. Nothing here is a rumor — it's the official breakdown of what Deluxe Edition owners will unlock in September.

🎵 Mayhem Collection — Confirmed Contents

Available September 18, 2026, included with the Deluxe Edition
  • Mayhem Mode — a new mode featuring The Joker and Harley Quinn as fully playable characters, each with their own unique abilities, gadgets, and takedowns
  • New Story Mission — The Joker and Harley Quinn break out of Arkham Asylum and cause chaos on the streets of Gotham City
  • Sinister Pack — seven (7) new suits, one per original playable character, plus five (5) new Batcave props and one (1) new Batmobile for the main game's open-world Gotham City

That's a meaningfully different shape of DLC compared to the cosmetic-only Legacy Collection that shipped at launch. Two new fully playable villains with their own combat kits, plus a dedicated story mission, puts the Mayhem Collection closer to a genuine content expansion than a cosmetic pack — which is probably why it's the piece of post-launch content players have been asking about most since May.

Gamescom 2026 Enters the Picture (Aug 26–30)

Gamescom 2026 runs August 26–30 at Koelnmesse in Cologne, with the Opening Night Live showcase kicking things off on August 25. According to Brick Fanatics, the LEGO Group has officially confirmed it will return to Gamescom for 2026 — and the timing is notable, because last year's Gamescom booth is exactly where LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight was first revealed to the world, alongside a large multi-franchise display covering Batman, One Piece, Super Mario, and more.

Brick Fanatics is careful to note that the LEGO Group's specific plans for this year's booth haven't been detailed publicly. But the outlet flags the Mayhem Collection — releasing just three weeks after the show closes — as “a likely candidate” for a fuller reveal, whether that's extended gameplay footage of Joker and Harley Quinn, a release trailer, or additional details on the Sinister Pack's suits and Batcave props. That's informed speculation from a LEGO-focused outlet, not an official announcement from WB Games or TT Games, so treat it as a reasonable bet rather than a lock.

What We Still Don't Know

  • Whether the Mayhem Collection will be sold as a standalone purchase for Standard Edition owners who skipped the Deluxe Edition, and if so, at what price — this hasn't been stated in any official materials to date
  • Exact mission length or level structure for the Arkham Asylum jailbreak story mission beyond the one-line description in the press release
  • Any gameplay footage of Joker or Harley Quinn's specific takedown animations beyond what appeared in the original May reveal trailer

If any of those gaps get filled, Gamescom week is the most logical window before the September 18 launch itself.

For now, the confirmed picture is solid: two new playable villains, a dedicated story mission, and a Sinister Pack of cosmetics, all arriving September 18 as part of the Deluxe Edition. Whether Gamescom adds a trailer or a price for Standard Edition owners remains to be seen — we'll update this page as soon as anything official lands.

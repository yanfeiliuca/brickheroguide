Searching for a working cheat code list? Here's the short version before you keep scrolling third-party sites: this game doesn't have one. Below is what actually exists instead, and why.

The Short Answer

LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight does not have a traditional cheat code system or an in-game "Extras" menu where you type a code to unlock invincibility, a Stud Multiplier, or a character. If you've seen a site promising a working cheat code list, treat it with skepticism — there is no confirmed, developer-supported cheat entry screen in this game.

Why This Game Has No Cheat Menu

Older LEGO games (LEGO Batman 2, LEGO Marvel Super Heroes, and others) let you collect Red Bricks that unlocked gameplay cheats — Stud Multipliers, Invincibility, Fast Build — activated from a menu. Legacy of the Dark Knight breaks from that formula: according to our own Collectibles Guide, all 23 Red Bricks in this game are purely cosmetic colour-scheme unlocks for costumes and vehicles. There is no Stud Multiplier Red Brick and no Invincibility toggle to find or buy. That single design change is the biggest reason "cheat code" searches for this game come up empty — the mechanic those codes used to unlock simply isn't in the game anymore.

In short: the cheat menu didn't get removed or hidden — the feature it used to unlock was redesigned out of the game entirely.

The Real Ways to Unlock Content

If you're searching for cheat codes because you want specific suits, vehicles, or bonus content, here's where that content actually comes from:

  • QR Redeem Codes — Physical LEGO Batman sets (76330–76333) include a QR code that unlocks bonus cosmetics like the Golden Batman suit and gold Batmobile variants through the game's official "Redeem Code" menu. See our QR Codes & Redeem Rewards Guide for the full redemption process.
  • Bat-Mite Store — 9 of the 23 Red Bricks (cosmetic colour schemes) are purchased directly with Studs you've already earned, no code required.
  • WayneTech Chips — Character ability and traversal upgrades come from WayneTech Caches and Falcone Fortunes, not codes. Our WayneTech Upgrades Guide covers the priority order.
  • Account-Linked Suits — Some suits unlock automatically for free by linking a WB Games or HBO Max account, not through a typed code. Check our Suits & Abilities Guide for the current list.

What Replaced the Classic Stud Multiplier Cheat?

In older LEGO games, a cheat code instantly maxed out your Stud Multiplier. Here, that same benefit is earned rather than typed in: GamesRadar+'s guide team confirms the multiplier is now a purchasable skill-tree perk called Hyper Combo — there are two upgrades sharing that name, both bought with Skill Bricks earned from story progress, and both increase your maximum Stud Multiplier and collection radius. There's no shortcut around earning the Skill Bricks first. See our WayneTech Upgrades Guide for where Hyper Combo sits in the priority order, and our Stud Farming Guide for the fastest way to put a maxed multiplier to use.

Is There a New Game Plus Instead?

No. Unlike some completionist-focused games that offset the lack of cheats with a New Game Plus carryover run, Legacy of the Dark Knight does not include a New Game Plus mode, per Game8's guide coverage. Replayability instead comes from the game's three difficulty tiers — Classic, Caped Crusader, and Dark Knight, covered in our Difficulty Modes Guide — and from chasing 100% completion district-by-district after the credits roll, rather than restarting the story with your unlocks intact.

What About PC Trainers?

Third-party Cheat Engine trainers for the PC version do exist on established trainer communities. If you choose to use one, treat it the way you'd treat any executable from a game-trainer site: stick to well-known, long-running trainer communities rather than random links, and back up your save file first, since trainers interacting with save data can occasionally cause instability.

Safety warning: never download a "cheat table" (.CT) file posted in an unverified Reddit thread or Discord server. Unverified cheat files are a common vector for hidden malware and crypto-miners. BrickHeroGuide does not host or endorse specific trainer downloads — this section exists purely to answer the "is it safe" question honestly.

If what you actually want is new-looking suits rather than gameplay cheats, PC players have a safer and more actively maintained option than trainers: the Nexus Mods cosmetic-modding scene. Creators there regularly publish comic-accurate suit reskins — see our roundup of one ongoing suit mod series covering Batman, Robin, and Gordon for a recent example.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a classic LEGO games cheat menu I'm missing?

No. Unlike previous LEGO titles, there is no Extras menu with code-entry fields. Every Red Brick in this game is cosmetic — see the Collectibles Guide for the full breakdown.

How do I get the Golden Batman suit?

It comes from redeeming the QR code included with specific physical LEGO Batman sets, not a typed cheat code. Full steps are in our Redeem Codes & QR Rewards Guide.

Will using a PC trainer get me banned?

The game has no online multiplayer component, which removes the anti-cheat ban risk that trainers typically carry in online games. The real risk isn't a ban — it's downloading an unverified file. Only use established trainer sources if you go this route.

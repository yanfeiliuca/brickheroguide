Mayhem Collection's Gamescom Debut Is Official: Trailer and Designer Interview Set for August 26

📅 August 20, 2026  |  ⏱ 5 min read

Yesterday we noted that a sold-out Gamescom 2026 with LEGO on the exhibitor list was "exactly the kind of setup that produces a trailer drop." Turns out that was less speculation than a forecast: Warner Bros. Games confirmed on August 19 that the Mayhem Collection DLC will make its first public appearance at Gamescom 2026, and a fresh trailer is now locked to a specific date and time. Here's everything that's actually confirmed, straight from WB Games and IGN.

WB Games Makes It Official

According to Brick Fanatics, the confirmation came from the Warner Bros. Games social media team, which posted a short teaser video on August 19 to promote the new content. The clip splices in footage of Heath Ledger's Joker from The Dark Knight, teasing Bruce Wayne about "the chaos to come" — a tonal nod rather than new gameplay. It's not yet clear whether the Mayhem Collection will actually be playable on the show floor at the LEGO booth, or whether the Gamescom presence is limited to video and press coverage.

Gamescom 2026 runs August 26–30 at Koelnmesse in Cologne, Germany, with the show floor opening to media and business attendees on the 26th before the public days begin, per GamesRadar+'s official schedule roundup.

IGN Gamescom Studio: Trailer Premiere and Designer Q&A

The bigger news is what's airing during IGN's own Gamescom programming. Brick Fanatics reports that IGN has confirmed a new trailer for the Mayhem Collection will premiere exclusively during its Gamescom Studio livestream on August 26. Alongside the trailer, IGN's coverage will include a deep-dive interview with LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight's Lead Designer Tim Spence and Advanced Designer Chris Payne, who are expected to go beyond the reveal trailer and talk through what players can actually expect from the DLC less than a month before it launches.

Why this matters: Every Mayhem Collection detail so far — Joker and Harley Quinn as playable characters, the Arkham Asylum breakout mission, the Sinister Pack — has come from the original May reveal trailer and a August-leaked Steam achievement list (see our achievements leak breakdown). August 26 is the first time WB Games and TT Games are expected to show anything beyond that.

What's Already Confirmed in the Sinister Pack

None of the Mayhem Collection's core contents changed with this announcement — this is still the official breakdown from WB Games' Deluxe Edition materials, not a rumor.

🎵 Mayhem Collection — Confirmed Contents

Available September 18, 2026, included with the Deluxe Edition
  • Mayhem Mode — The Joker and Harley Quinn become fully playable, each with their own abilities, gadgets, and takedowns
  • New Story Mission — an Arkham Asylum breakout mission that spills chaos into Gotham City
  • Sinister Pack — a new suit for each of the game's 7 existing playable characters, 5 new Batcave decorations, and 1 new Batmobile skin

Pricing for Standard Edition Owners

Deluxe Edition owners get the Mayhem Collection automatically the moment it releases on September 18. Brick Fanatics reports that players who bought the Standard Edition can upgrade for £21.99 / $24.99, which unlocks both the Mayhem Collection and the existing Legacy Collection cosmetic pack in one purchase.

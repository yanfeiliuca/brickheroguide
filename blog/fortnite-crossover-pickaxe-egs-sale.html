LEGO Batman Legacy's Free Fortnite Pickaxe Is Still Live — Plus a 20% Epic Games Store Sale

Published: July 27, 2026 · 6 min read

One detail about LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight that hasn't gotten much attention on its own: buying the game on the Epic Games Store still comes with a free crossover reward for a completely different game. We checked the Epic Games Store listing directly today, and the offer — a free Golden Batarang Knucks Pickaxe and matching loading screen for Fortnite — is still active more than two months after launch. The store is also running a 20% sale on the whole LEGO Batman lineup right now, so this is a good moment to lay out exactly what the crossover gets you, how to actually claim it, and what the current pricing looks like.

What You Actually Get: A Pickaxe and a Loading Screen for Fortnite

The reward is two cosmetic items for Fortnite, not for LEGO Batman itself: the Golden Batarang Knucks Pickaxe and the Bricks and Batarangs Loading Screen. Both are gifts-with-purchase (GWP) tied to buying LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on Epic Games Store — they're not sold separately and, as of today, aren't listed in the Fortnite Item Shop. Once unlocked, they're yours to keep permanently; there's no time limit on using them after they've been added to your account.

The catch: These items only work inside Fortnite. You can't equip the Golden Batarang Knucks Pickaxe in LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight — it's a cross-promotional reward for Epic's other game, not an in-game unlock for this one.

How to Claim It

According to Brick Fanatics' original coverage of the promotion, the pickaxe and loading screen were made available to anyone who pre-ordered LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight ahead of its May 22, 2026 launch, as well as to anyone who bought the game on Epic Games Store afterward as a standard purchase. The one requirement that trips people up: you have to be signed into the same Epic Games account when you buy LEGO Batman and when you play Fortnite for the items to show up. If you use separate accounts for the two games, the reward won't carry over.

Players who bought the Deluxe Edition also got early access to the base game three days before launch, on May 19, 2026 — a separate perk from the Fortnite crossover, but part of the same pre-order push Epic and TT Games ran together in the lead-up to release.

The Current Epic Games Store Deal

As of today, Epic Games Store has the entire LEGO Batman lineup discounted 20%, with the sale running through August 10, 2026 at 11:00 AM:

ProductRegular PriceSale Price
Base Game$69.99$55.99

The Deluxe Edition Upgrade is the same add-on covered in our Deluxe Edition breakdown — it layers the Legacy Collection's launch-day cosmetic packs plus a season pass covering the upcoming Mayhem Collection DLC on top of the base game. Buying any of these three products during the sale window still triggers the Fortnite pickaxe and loading screen GWP, since the offer is tied to owning the game on Epic Games Store rather than to paying full price for it.

Worth knowing: This is an Epic Games Store-specific sale. We checked Steam's store page for the same game today and found no equivalent discount currently listed there — base game and Deluxe Edition are both showing full price on Steam as of this writing.

Should You Buy on Epic Games Store Just for the Pickaxe?

If you're already planning to buy LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight on PC and don't have a strong platform preference, the current 20% discount plus a free permanent Fortnite cosmetic makes Epic Games Store the more complete deal of the two PC storefronts right now, at least until Steam runs its own promotion. If you're a committed Steam user, the difference is really just the pickaxe and loading screen — neither one affects anything in LEGO Batman itself, so it comes down to whether you or someone in your household plays Fortnite and would get use out of them.

Either way, the sale window closes August 10, and the Fortnite items are worth grabbing while the offer is still listed on the store page — Epic hasn't given any indication of when, or whether, this particular crossover promotion will be pulled.
